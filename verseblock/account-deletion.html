VerseBlock Deletion and Cancellation

Last updated: 5 September 2026.

Delete your account

Open Account → Delete account. Enter DELETE and your current password, then confirm. You do not need to wait for a subscription to expire.

The service verifies your identity and deletes your Supabase account and basic profile. The app clears local account state and requests alarm cancellation. Failed requests are not shown as successful. If iOS cannot cancel an alarm, follow the error and stop or remove it.

Local previews delete demo data only. Sign-out is not cloud-account deletion. Other installations and older backups are not remotely erased. No app-saved audio or transcript history exists.

If you cannot access the app, email privacy@sodhera.com with your account email and request. Do not send passwords or codes. We may verify identity. You can also request associated support or RevenueCat customer-record deletion. Legally required records and provider backups may follow separate retention periods; see the Privacy Policy.

Cancel renewal separately

Account deletion and uninstallation do not cancel Apple subscriptions.

  1. Open Account → Manage subscription, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  2. Select VerseBlock.
  3. Cancel renewal and check the expiry shown by Apple.

Manage subscriptions · Apple cancellation help · Refund help

Local preview and Test Store purchases are free simulations. Apple controls refunds and its transaction records.
