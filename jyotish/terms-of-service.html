2. Description of the Service

Jyotish baje is a Vedic astrology application that provides:

  • Kundali (Birth Chart) Generation: Computation of Vedic birth charts using sidereal planetary positions with Lahiri ayanamsa.
  • Rashifal (Horoscope): Daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly horoscope readings based on your birth chart.
  • Panchanga: Traditional Vedic almanac information including tithi, yoga, karana, and nakshatra.
  • Bikram Sambat Calendar (Patro): A Nepali calendar with cultural and religious events.
  • Family Astrology (Parivar): Ability to add family members and generate astrological readings for the entire family.
  • AI Chatbot ("Pandit-ji"): An artificial intelligence-powered conversational assistant that answers questions about Vedic astrology, vastu, gemstones, colors, and related topics.
  • Voice Input: Optional voice-to-text input using on-device speech recognition.
  • Bilingual Support: Full support for English and Nepali (Devanagari).
