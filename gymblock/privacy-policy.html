Privacy Policy

GymBlock
Effective Date: October 8, 2026
Last Updated: November 12, 2026

Sodhera Intelligence Private Limited ("Sodhera," "we," "us," or "our") operates the GymBlock application for iPhone (the "App"). GymBlock helps you keep your phone away between sets, time your rests, log your sets, and see like-for-like strength progress. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the App, why we collect it, where it is stored, who processes it on our behalf, and the choices you have. By using GymBlock, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

We are committed to transparency and to safeguarding your personal data. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with any part of it, please do not use the App.

At a glance. Everything you log is kept on your iPhone and synced to your account in our own database. If you add a friend as your GymBuddy, they see your first name and your workout days, and we send push notifications about each other's workouts (Sections 1.6 and 4). If you post a feature request or a comment, every GymBlock user can read it with your first name (Section 1.7). We record how the App is used (screens, taps, workout events) in that same database and with PostHog, the product analytics service that processes those events for us. We advertise GymBlock on TikTok, so the App tells TikTok when it is installed and opened and when an account, a free trial, or a subscription starts, to measure those ads (Section 1.5); never your name, email address, workouts, or anything you type. There are no ads in the App, we never use the advertising identifier, and your data is never sold. Blocking uses Apple's Screen Time: the apps you choose stay on your iPhone in a form Apple keeps unreadable to us, so we never learn which apps they are or how you use them.

If the Apple or Google identity you choose already belongs to a GymBlock account, you are signed in to that account and the workouts on your iPhone are added to it. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth on our behalf. We never see or store a password.

1.4 Product Analytics

GymBlock records how the App is used. The events are written by the App to our own database (hosted by Supabase), and the same events are also sent to PostHog, a product analytics service that processes them on our behalf (see Section 6). They are never sent to TikTok or any other advertiser; TikTok receives only the short list in Section 1.5. We want to be clear that this logging is extensive:

  • Screens: every screen you view and how long you stay on it.
  • Interactions: every button tap, toggle, and choice you make, including the option you selected.
  • Blocking: whether Screen Time access was allowed, how many apps, categories, and websites you chose (counts only, never which ones), and how many one-minute passes were used.
  • Workouts: when a workout starts and finishes; each set logged, edited, or deleted; each rest started, skipped, or completed, with durations.
  • GymBuddy and sharing: codes shared or entered, friends added or removed, nudges sent, and where you chose to share your streak card (for example Instagram, Messages, or Save), never the friend's name or the message.
  • Onboarding: each onboarding step you reach, the answer you give, and where you stop if you do not finish.
  • Purchases: paywall views, purchase attempts, completions, failures, and restores.
  • Account: sign-in and sign-out.
  • Errors: failures the App detects.

Each event carries a timestamp, the install ID, a session ID, your account ID when you are signed in, the App version, the device model, and the iOS version. In PostHog the same events also carry your locale, time zone, and screen size, and PostHog receives your IP address when an event arrives; we configure PostHog not to work out your location from it. Events never include your name, your email address, or anything you type.

We use these events to understand which parts of the App are used, where people get stuck, whether rests and alerts work as intended, and how to improve the App. We do not use them for advertising, do not share them with advertisers or data brokers, and do not combine them with data about you from other companies. There is no separate analytics switch inside the App at this time; you may object to this processing or ask us to delete your events at any time by emailing admin@sodhera.com. Deleting your account deletes the events in our database; the copy held by PostHog is deleted when you ask us (see Section 9).

1.5 Advertising Measurement (TikTok)

We advertise GymBlock on TikTok. To learn which of those ads lead to people installing and subscribing, the App includes TikTok's Business SDK, which sends the following to TikTok:

  • Events: the App was installed, opened, or opened again on a later day; a new GymBlock account was created (not when you sign back in); a free trial started; a paid subscription started, with its price and currency.
  • With each event: the identifier Apple gives our apps on your iPhone (the "identifier for vendor," which is the same only across our own apps), your IP address, the device model, iOS and App version, your language and region setting, and a technical description of the App and device (user agent).

TikTok does not receive your name, your email address, your account ID, your workouts or answers, the apps you block, anything you type, or the analytics events in Section 1.4. We do not read the advertising identifier (IDFA) and do not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so TikTok never receives it. We have also switched off the parts of TikTok's SDK that would report device details such as when your iPhone was last restarted, read text on screen, observe purchases on their own, or send crash reports.

Installs from TikTok ads are also reported through SKAdNetwork, Apple's attribution system, which tells TikTok that an ad led to an install without telling TikTok or us who you are.

TikTok uses these events to measure our ads and to show them to people more likely to find GymBlock useful. It handles them under its own privacy policy and business terms.

1.6 GymBuddy (Training With a Friend)

GymBuddy is optional. You become someone's GymBuddy only when one of you enters the other's code. Once connected:

  • What your friend sees: your first name (the first word of the name in your profile, or "Your friend" if you left it empty), the days you finished a workout in the last ten weeks, the name of your last workout and when you finished it, and your streak.
  • What your friend's notifications say: when you finish a workout, a push such as "Alex just finished Push. 52 min · 18 sets." (how often depends on their setting); when your streak is about to end, a reminder that they can nudge you; and when you nudge them, that you did.
  • What your friend never sees: your weights, reps, exercises, body measurements, onboarding answers, the apps you block, your email address, or your subscription.
  • The two weeks of Pro: when a friend enters your code (or you enter theirs), each of you gets two weeks of GymBlock Pro. We give it through RevenueCat under your account ID (your two weeks start when your current paid plan ends).
  • Ending it: Remove a friend in the App at any time, and they stop seeing your weeks; the two weeks of Pro stay. Deleting your account removes all of your GymBuddy connections.

1.7 Feature Requests (Request a Feature)

The feature board (Settings → Help → Request a feature) is optional. It lists what people have asked GymBlock to do next, and every signed-in GymBlock user can read it, vote, and comment.

  • What everyone sees: the words of each request and comment you post, your first name (the first word of the name in your profile; nothing if you left it empty), when you posted it, and how many votes and comments a request has. Nobody sees your account ID, email address, workouts, or who voted.
  • Posting: don't write anything about yourself or anyone else you would not want everyone to read. A request or comment holds up to 300 characters.
  • Report and hide: Report removes a request or comment from your board at once, and anything three people report is hidden from everyone until we look at it. Hide this person removes everything an account wrote from your board. We may remove posts and stop an account from posting (see the Terms of Service).
  • Deleting: you can delete your own requests and comments at any time. Deleting your account deletes your requests, comments, votes, reports, and hides. If an account that was never saved with Apple or Google is deleted automatically for being unused (Section 9), its requests and comments stay on the board with no name.

What you write on the board never goes to PostHog or TikTok; our analytics record only that something was posted, voted on, or reported.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  1. Providing the App: storing your splits, workouts, and sets; keeping a copy on your iPhone and in your account; and restoring your data when you sign in on another iPhone.
  2. Timing rests and alerts: your rest length and alert preferences drive the rest timer, local notifications, and the Lock Screen Live Activity. These run on your iPhone.
  3. Showing progress: like-for-like progress is calculated from the sets you log, comparing the same exercise within the same split with reps or load held constant. The "time on your phone" figure is calculated from your onboarding answers. These are calculations from what you entered, not measurements of you.
  4. Subscriptions: confirming whether GymBlock Pro is active, restoring purchases, and giving the two weeks of Pro that come with GymBuddy.
  5. GymBuddy and push notifications: showing your friends' weeks and sending the push notifications in Section 4.
  6. Understanding and improving the App: the product analytics described in Section 1.4.
  7. Measuring our ads: the TikTok events described in Section 1.5.
  8. Support, security, and legal obligations: answering your questions, verifying requests, protecting accounts and systems, complying with applicable law, and enforcing our Terms of Service.

We do not show ads in the App, sell your data, or rent it. Apart from the TikTok events in Section 1.5, which measure our own ads, we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing. We do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

3. App Blocking and Apple Screen Time

GymBlock blocks the apps you choose while a workout runs, using Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity). Here is exactly what that involves:

  • Permission. GymBlock asks for Screen Time access for your own iPhone. iOS shows its own prompt, and you approve it with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode. You can turn it off at any time in iOS Settings → Screen Time, and deleting GymBlock removes it.
  • Choosing apps. You choose apps, app categories, and websites in Apple's own picker. Apple gives GymBlock only opaque tokens for your choice. GymBlock cannot turn those tokens into app names, icons, or websites, and never learns what you picked.
  • Where the choice is kept. The tokens are stored only on your iPhone, in GymBlock and its Screen Time extensions. They are never synced to your account, sent to us, or included in analytics. Deleting your account in the App clears them.
  • When apps are blocked. From Start workout to Finish, iOS shows a GymBlock screen over the apps you chose. iOS keeps the block if GymBlock is closed, and removes it if GymBlock is deleted.
  • The one-minute pass. With Strict mode off, the block screen offers one minute per exercise. During that minute, iOS tells GymBlock's extension when a minute's use of the blocked apps has been reached so the block can return; iOS does not tell GymBlock which app was used or what you did in it.
  • What we record. Only the counts listed in Section 1.4: whether access was allowed, how many apps, categories, and websites were chosen, and how many passes were used.

GymBlock does not read which apps are installed on your iPhone, which apps you open, your Screen Time reports, or anything you do inside other apps. GymBlock uses Screen Time only to block apps you chose for yourself; it is not a parental control app and cannot manage anyone else's device.

4. Notifications and Live Activities

  • Local notifications: GymBlock can notify you when a rest ends and remind you if a workout has been left running. These are scheduled by the App on your iPhone, and their content never leaves your device.
  • Push notifications: if you allow notifications and are signed in, the App registers a push token with our database. Our server (a Supabase function) then sends pushes through Apple Push Notification service: when a friend joins with your code, nudges you, or finishes a workout (every workout, rarely, or none: choose in Settings → Buddy workouts or on the GymBuddy page), when a friend's streak is about to end, and occasionally a message from us about GymBlock, such as a new feature. The text of each push is stored in our database with the time it was sent. The token is removed when you log out or delete your account.
  • Turning them off: iOS Settings → Notifications → GymBlock turns off all of them.
  • Live Activity: during a workout, GymBlock can show the current rest or set on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, rendered by iOS from data already on your iPhone.

5. Where Your Data Lives

5.1 On Your iPhone

A local copy of your profile, settings, splits, workouts, and sets is stored in the App's sandboxed storage on your iPhone, so the App works offline and responds instantly. Session tokens and the install ID are also stored on the device. Analytics events are queued on the device and sent when a connection is available.

5.2 In Your Account

The same data is synced to your account in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase. Every row is associated with your account ID and protected by Row Level Security, so only requests authenticated as you can read or change your rows. Deleting your account deletes these rows (see Section 9).

GymBlock is free to use. 8. Data Security

We implement the following measures to protect your data:

  • TLS encryption for all data in transit between the App and our providers
  • Row Level Security in our database so that only the account owner's authenticated session can read or modify their rows
  • No passwords held by us; sign-in relies on Apple and Google
  • Encryption at rest on Supabase infrastructure
  • Least-privilege access to production data within Sodhera, limited to what support and maintenance require

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data breach notification. If a breach of your personal data is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you without undue delay (where feasible, within 72 hours of becoming aware of it), explain what happened and what we are doing about it, and report the breach to the relevant supervisory authority where the law requires.

9. Data Retention

  • While your account exists: we keep your account information, profile, settings, splits, workouts, sets, GymBuddy connections, push messages, device records, subscription snapshot, and analytics events so the App can work and restore your data. Feature requests and comments stay on the board until you delete them or your account, or we remove them.
  • Log out: logging out ends your session on that iPhone, removes its push token, and clears the local copy from the device; your account and its data stay, and come back when you sign in again. An account you have not saved with Apple or Google cannot be signed in to again, so the App asks you to save it before you log out.
  • Unused accounts: an account that was never saved with Apple or Google, has no finished workout, and has not been opened for 30 days is deleted automatically, with all of its data except any feature requests and comments it posted, which stay on the board with no name.
  • Delete account: Settings → Delete account in the App deletes your account and all of its data from our database immediately, including your GymBuddy connections, and wipes the local copy from that iPhone. Analytics events and device records stay in our database without your account ID (they keep the random install ID). If you saved your account with Apple or Google, you can also email admin@sodhera.com from that email address. See User Data Deletion.
  • Analytics in PostHog: kept for as long as our PostHog plan retains them. Deleting your account in the App does not reach PostHog on its own; email admin@sodhera.com and we will delete the events linked to your account there within 30 days.
  • Ad measurement events at TikTok: kept by TikTok under its own retention rules. They are not linked to your account, so deleting your account does not reach them; the identifier for vendor they carry changes once GymBlock and any other apps of ours are deleted from your iPhone.
  • Backups: encrypted database backups held by our hosting provider roll off within 30 days after deletion.
  • Aggregated statistics: we may keep statistics that cannot identify anyone (for example, how many workouts were logged in a month).
  • Legal records: we may retain limited records where the law requires it, for example transaction records needed for tax or accounting.
