Privacy Policy

GymBlock
Effective Date: October 8, 2026
Last Updated: October 8, 2026

Sodhera Intelligence Private Limited ("Sodhera," "we," "us," or "our") operates the GymBlock application for iPhone (the "App"). GymBlock helps you keep your phone away between sets, time your rests, log your sets, and see like-for-like strength progress. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the App, why we collect it, where it is stored, who processes it on our behalf, and the choices you have. By using GymBlock, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

We are committed to transparency and to safeguarding your personal data. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with any part of it, please do not use the App.

At a glance. Everything you log is kept on your iPhone and synced to your account in our own database. We record how the App is used (screens, taps, workout events) in that same database, and only there. No advertising SDKs, no cross-app tracking, and your data is never sold. "Blocking" other apps is simulated: GymBlock does not control, monitor, or read other apps.

1.4 Product Analytics

GymBlock records how the App is used. This is first-party analytics: the events are written by the App to our own database (hosted by Supabase) and nowhere else. We do not use any third-party analytics, attribution, or advertising SDK. We want to be clear that this logging is extensive:

  • Screens: every screen you view and how long you stay on it.
  • Interactions: every button tap, toggle, and choice you make, including the option you selected.
  • Workouts: when a workout starts, pauses, resumes, and finishes; each set logged, edited, or deleted; each rest started, skipped, or completed, with durations.
  • Onboarding: each onboarding step you reach, the answer you give, and where you stop if you do not finish.
  • Purchases: paywall views, purchase attempts, completions, failures, and restores.
  • Account: sign-in and sign-out.
  • Errors: failures the App detects.

Each event carries a timestamp, the install ID, a session ID, your account ID when you are signed in, the App version, the device model, and the iOS version. Events are linked to your account so they can be deleted with it.

We use these events to understand which parts of the App are used, where people get stuck, whether rests and alerts work as intended, and how to improve the App. We do not use them for advertising, do not share them with advertisers or data brokers, and do not combine them with data about you from other companies. There is no separate analytics switch inside the App at this time; you may object to this processing or ask us to delete your events at any time by emailing admin@sodhera.com, and deleting your account deletes the events linked to it.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  1. Providing the App: storing your splits, workouts, and sets; keeping a copy on your iPhone and in your account; and restoring your data when you sign in on another iPhone.
  2. Timing rests and alerts: your rest length and alert preferences drive the rest timer, local notifications, and the Lock Screen Live Activity. These run on your iPhone.
  3. Showing progress: like-for-like progress is calculated from the sets you log, comparing the same exercise within the same split with reps or load held constant. The "time on your phone" figure is calculated from your onboarding answers. These are calculations from what you entered, not measurements of you.
  4. Subscriptions: confirming whether GymBlock Pro is active and restoring purchases.
  5. Understanding and improving the App: the product analytics described in Section 1.4.
  6. Support, security, and legal obligations: answering your questions, verifying requests, protecting accounts and systems, complying with applicable law, and enforcing our Terms of Service.

We do not use your data for advertising, do not sell it, do not rent it, and do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

3. App Blocking: What GymBlock Actually Does

GymBlock's purpose is to keep your phone away between sets, and the App talks about "blocking" apps. In the current version of the App, blocking is simulated and representational. When a workout is running, the App shows a block screen and treats the apps you chose during onboarding as blocked, but:

  • GymBlock does not control, restrict, monitor, or read other apps on your iPhone.
  • GymBlock does not use Apple's Screen Time, Family Controls, or Managed Settings frameworks, and never asks for those permissions.
  • GymBlock receives no information about which apps are installed on your iPhone or how you use them.
  • The list of "apps to block" you pick during onboarding is stored only as your answer to that question.

If a future version adds real enforcement, we will update this Privacy Policy, describe exactly what the feature can and cannot see, and ask for any required system permission at that time.

4. Notifications and Live Activities

  • Local notifications: GymBlock can notify you when a rest ends and remind you if a workout has been left running. These are scheduled by the App on your iPhone; we run no push notification server, and notification content never leaves your device. Turn them off in iOS Settings → Notifications → GymBlock.
  • Live Activity: during a workout, GymBlock can show the current rest or set on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, rendered by iOS from data already on your iPhone.

5. Where Your Data Lives

5.1 On Your iPhone

A local copy of your profile, settings, splits, workouts, and sets is stored in the App's sandboxed storage on your iPhone, so the App works offline and responds instantly. Session tokens and the install ID are also stored on the device. Analytics events are queued on the device and sent when a connection is available.

5.2 In Your Account

The same data is synced to your account in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase. Every row is associated with your account ID and protected by Row Level Security, so only requests authenticated as you can read or change your rows. Deleting your account deletes these rows (see Section 9).

8. Data Security

We implement the following measures to protect your data:

  • TLS encryption for all data in transit between the App and our providers
  • Row Level Security in our database so that only the account owner's authenticated session can read or modify their rows
  • No passwords held by us; sign-in relies on Apple and Google
  • Encryption at rest on Supabase infrastructure
  • Least-privilege access to production data within Sodhera, limited to what support and maintenance require

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data breach notification. If a breach of your personal data is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you without undue delay (where feasible, within 72 hours of becoming aware of it), explain what happened and what we are doing about it, and report the breach to the relevant supervisory authority where the law requires.

9. Data Retention

  • While your account exists: we keep your account information, profile, settings, splits, workouts, sets, device records, subscription snapshot, and analytics events so the App can work and restore your data.
  • Log out: logging out ends your session on that iPhone but keeps your account and its data, and keeps the local copy on the device, so you can sign in again.
  • Delete account: Settings → Delete account in the App deletes your account and all of its data from our database immediately and wipes the local copy from that iPhone. You can also email admin@sodhera.com from the account's email address. See User Data Deletion.
  • Backups: encrypted database backups held by our hosting provider roll off within 30 days after deletion.
  • Aggregated statistics: we may keep statistics that cannot identify anyone (for example, how many workouts were logged in a month).
  • Legal records: we may retain limited records where the law requires it, for example transaction records needed for tax or accounting.
