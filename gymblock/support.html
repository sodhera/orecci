Subscriptions and Refunds

GymBlock Pro is billed by Apple through the App Store. To cancel, open iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions and select GymBlock. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If a purchase does not appear after reinstalling, use Restore purchases in the app.

Privacy & Data Requests

You can delete your account and all of its data from Settings → Delete account in the app. For data export, deletion by email, or any other privacy request, write to us from the email address linked to your account. See User Data Deletion for details.
