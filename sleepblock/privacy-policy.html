Privacy Policy

SleepBlock
Effective Date: July 12, 2026
Last Updated: August 21, 2026

Sodhera Intelligence Private Limited ("Sodhera," "we," "us," or "our") operates the SleepBlock mobile application (the "App"). SleepBlock is a bedtime commitment app that helps you set a sleep schedule, log your nights, and build a healthier sleep rhythm. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use the App. By using SleepBlock, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

We are committed to transparency and to safeguarding your personal data. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with any part of it, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you set up and use SleepBlock, you may voluntarily provide the following information:

Data Category Specific Data Purpose
Account Details Email address, and a password (for email sign-up), or the identity token returned by Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google Create and secure your account and let you sign in across sessions
Profile Your first name (or preferred name) as entered during onboarding or edited in Profile Personalize the app greeting and your profile
Onboarding Questionnaire What gets in the way of your sleep, which apps keep you up at night, your usual bedtime, and your usual wake time Tailor your sleep plan and set your initial sleep schedule and suggested apps to block
Sleep Sessions Nights you log in-app with "Sleep Now" and "Wake up" — start time, end time, duration, and a computed sleep score Show your last-night summary, streaks, seven-night chart, and history
Sleep Schedule Your chosen target bedtime and wake time Display the bedtime countdown and measure nights against your goal
Blocked Apps Selection The set of apps and categories you choose to shield, selected through Apple's Screen Time picker Enable optional sleep-enforcement features that shield selected apps during sleep
Preferences App settings and choices you make (such as theme and Apple Health sync toggle) Customize your app experience
About Apple Screen Time selections: When you choose which apps to block, Apple's Family Controls framework returns only opaque, privacy-preserving tokens. We never receive the names, icons, or bundle identifiers of the apps you select, and we cannot see how you use them. These tokens are meaningful only to the operating system on your own device.

1.2 Apple Health (HealthKit) Data

Apple Health integration is entirely optional. If you choose to connect it from the Profile prompt or Settings, and only after you grant permission, SleepBlock may:

  • Read your sleep analysis history from Apple Health, so imported nights can appear alongside nights you log in-app.
  • Write nights you log in SleepBlock back to Apple Health as sleep-analysis samples.

Health data accessed through HealthKit stays on your device and, if you enable it, syncs only through Apple's own iCloud/Health mechanisms under your Apple ID. In accordance with Apple's requirements, we do not use HealthKit data for advertising or marketing, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties. You can revoke Health access at any time in the iOS Settings app or the Apple Health app.

1.4 Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your sleep or health data to serve advertising.
  • We do not collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). The App never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt and never asks for permission to track you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create your account and authenticate you securely;
  • Build and display your personalized sleep plan, schedule, and history;
  • Compute your sleep summaries, scores, streaks, and charts;
  • Merge and de-duplicate nights logged in-app with nights imported from Apple Health, so a night is never double-counted;
  • Enable optional sleep-enforcement features that shield the apps you select;
  • Process and manage your subscription and free trial;
  • Respond to your support requests;
  • Maintain the security, integrity, and reliability of the App; and
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. How Your Information Is Stored

SleepBlock is designed to be local-first.

  • On your device: Your profile, sleep sessions, sleep schedule, blocked-apps selection, and preferences are stored locally on your device. Authentication tokens are stored securely in the iOS Keychain.
  • Sleep data is not stored on our servers. Today, your logged sleep sessions are not uploaded to or synced through a Sodhera server.
  • Account identity: Your account credentials and email are handled by our authentication provider (see Section 4) to give you a stable sign-in. This account acts as an identity gate and is kept separate from your on-device sleep data.

5. Advertising Measurement

We advertise SleepBlock on TikTok. To know whether those ads actually lead anywhere, the App includes the TikTok Business SDK, which reports a small number of milestone events to TikTok. This is measurement only — it tells us how many people who saw an ad went on to install the app or subscribe. It does not involve your sleep or health data, and SleepBlock itself shows no ads.

5.1 What the App Sends to TikTok

  • Technical data included with each request: a device identifier (Apple's identifier for vendors, which is specific to our apps on your device), your IP address, the app version, your locale, your OS version, a user agent string, and the App's App Tracking Transparency status.
  • Three milestone events: when an account is created ("Registration"), when a free trial is started ("StartTrial"), and when a subscription is purchased ("Subscribe," which includes the plan's price and currency).
  • Install and app-launch events, which the SDK reports on its own.

5.2 What the App Never Sends to TikTok

These are firm commitments, not defaults we could quietly change:

  • No advertising identifier (IDFA). The App never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt and never requests permission to track you.
  • No email address, phone number, name, or account identifier. We have deliberately chosen not to use TikTok's identity-matching (advanced matching) feature, so TikTok receives nothing that identifies you personally.
  • No sleep sessions, sleep schedules, blocked-app selections, or Apple Health data. None of it leaves your device for advertising purposes, ever.

Because we send no advertising identifier and no personal identifiers, this measurement does not link you to data collected by other companies' apps or websites, and we do not use it for tracking as Apple defines that term.

6. Payments and Subscriptions

SleepBlock is offered as a subscription with a free trial. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store and managed via RevenueCat. We never receive or store your full payment card details. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID, and subscription management, cancellation, and refunds are handled through your Apple account settings, subject to Apple's terms.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • With service providers listed in Section 4, strictly to operate the App;
  • For legal reasons, if required by law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request;
  • To protect rights and safety, where necessary to prevent fraud, abuse, or harm; and
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, in which case we will notify you and this Policy will continue to apply to your information.

8. Data Retention

Data stored locally on your device remains until you delete it in the App, sign out, or uninstall the App. Account information held by our authentication provider is retained for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (see Section 10), we delete or anonymize the associated account data, except where we must retain limited records to comply with legal obligations.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) for network communication, secure storage of authentication tokens in the iOS Keychain, and reliance on reputable providers with strong security practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Within the App you can:

  • Edit your name and account email in Profile;
  • Enable or disable Apple Health sync at any time;
  • Change or clear your blocked-apps selection and sleep schedule;
  • Sign out, which removes your session from the device; and
  • Delete data stored on your device by uninstalling the App.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data, email us at admin@sodhera.com. You may also revoke Apple Health permissions at any time from the iOS Settings or Apple Health app.

11. Children's Privacy

SleepBlock is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

12. International Users

Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers. By using the App, you understand that your information may be transferred and processed in this way.
