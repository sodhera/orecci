Sodhera Intelligence Private Limited ("Sodhera," "we," "us," or "our") operates the JournalBlock mobile application (the "App"). JournalBlock is a morning journal app that holds selected apps on your phone shut until you have written the day's page. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use the App. By using JournalBlock, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

We are committed to transparency and to safeguarding your personal data. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with any part of it, please do not use the App.

1.2 Your Journal and End-to-End Encryption

Your journal entries are stored on your device. Backup is entirely optional and is switched on by you, from the Backup screen in Settings. If you never turn it on, nothing you write ever leaves your phone.

When you do turn backup on, JournalBlock uses end-to-end encryption. In practice this means:

  • A random encryption key is generated on your device and never leaves it in readable form. It is stored in the iOS Keychain, and travels to your other devices through Apple's own iCloud Keychain under your Apple ID.
  • Every journal entry is encrypted on your device before it is uploaded. Your prompts, sittings, preferences, and onboarding answers are encrypted the same way, in a single blob — a reworded prompt can be as personal as the answer to it.
  • Our servers receive ciphertext only, plus the day an entry belongs to and the time it was last changed. We cannot read your entries, your prompts, or your settings, and neither can our hosting provider.
  • The encryption key itself is stored only in a form sealed under a recovery phrase that we never receive and never store.
We cannot recover your journal for you. Because the key never reaches us in a usable form, there is no password reset, no support request, and no administrative override that can decrypt your entries. This is a deliberate design choice, and the trade-off is real: if you lose access to both your devices and your recovery phrase, your backed-up entries cannot be recovered by anyone, including us. See Section 6 of the Terms of Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Build and display your personalized plan and your daily sittings;
  • Store and display your entries, history, streak, and seven-day chain;
  • Determine what the day owes, and drive the Home Screen widget and the shield accordingly;
  • Enable the optional blocking feature that shields the apps you select;
  • Deliver the optional wake alarm and reminder notifications you turn on;
  • Store and restore your encrypted backup, if you enable it;
  • Process and manage your subscription and free trial;
  • Respond to your support requests;
  • Maintain the security, integrity, and reliability of the App; and
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. How Your Information Is Stored

JournalBlock is designed to be local-first.

  • On your device: Your entries, sittings, prompts, blocked-apps selection, and preferences are stored locally on your device. Authentication tokens and your encryption key are stored securely in the iOS Keychain.
  • On our servers, only if you enable backup: encrypted entry blobs, an encrypted settings blob, and the sealed form of your encryption key — none of which we can read. We also store the day each entry belongs to and when it was last updated, so that your devices can stay in sync.
  • Account identity: Your email address and name are handled by our authentication provider (see Section 4) to give you a stable sign-in. This account acts as an identity gate and is kept separate from the contents of your journal.
  • Never uploaded: your blocked-apps selection, which remains on your device as opaque system tokens.

Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID, and subscription management, cancellation, and refunds are handled through your Apple account settings, subject to Apple's terms.

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • With service providers listed in Section 4, strictly to operate the App;
  • For legal reasons, if required by law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request;

We note that even under legal compulsion we cannot produce the contents of your journal, because we do not hold the means to decrypt it.

7. Data Retention

Data stored locally on your device remains until you delete it in the App, sign out, or uninstall the App. Encrypted backup data and account information are retained for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (see Section 8), your profile, your encrypted entries, your encrypted settings, and your stored key material are deleted, except where we must retain limited records to comply with legal obligations.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Within the App you can:

  • Edit your name in Settings;
  • Turn encrypted backup on or off, and issue a fresh recovery phrase;
  • Add, retime, rename, or delete any sitting, and edit every prompt;
  • Change or clear your blocked-apps selection at any time;
  • Turn strict mode, the wake alarm, and reminders on or off;
  • Sign out, which removes your session from the device but leaves your journal in place;
  • Delete your account from within the App — Settings → Account → Delete Account. This removes your account and all server-side data associated with it, and then erases the journal on your device. It cannot be undone; and
  • Delete data stored on your device by uninstalling the App.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription — Apple handles billing, and you must cancel in your Apple ID account settings. To request access to or correction of your account data, email us at admin@sodhera.com.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including end-to-end encryption of journal content using AES-GCM with keys generated on your device, encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) for all network communication, secure storage of authentication tokens and encryption keys in the iOS Keychain, row-level security so that no account can read another's rows, and reliance on reputable providers with strong security practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
