Data Privacy

User Data Deletion

We respect your privacy. Here is how you can delete your user data.

Delete from the App

You can delete your user data and account directly from within the Speaking Coach app. Simply navigate to your profile or settings within the app and select the option to delete your profile/account.

Contact Support

Alternatively, if you are unable to delete your account from the app, or if you want to ensure that all your data is permanently removed, you can request data deletion by contacting our support team via email.
