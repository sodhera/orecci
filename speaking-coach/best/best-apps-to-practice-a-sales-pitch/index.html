Two markets, one search

Search for "sales pitch practice app" and most results are platforms sold to sales teams: per-seat pricing, CRM integrations, a demo call before you see a price. That's the right answer for a sales manager and the wrong one for a founder pitching on Friday, a new SDR practicing on their own time, or a student with a pitch competition.

So we split this page: apps a single person can download today, then platforms for teams. We opened each tool's own site or App Store page in November 2026 and state only what we saw. We make Speaking Coach, which is in the first group.

For one person

SalesDojo. Its App Store listing describes AI buyers that "talk, interrupt, and ask tough questions", with personas from friendly to skeptical, in prospecting, demo, upsell and renewal scenarios. After each call you get scores on discovery, listening, objection handling, closing, confidence and product knowledge, with coaching tips. Plans listed were $49.99, $99.99 and $199.99; the page doesn't say whether they recur. English only. Best for: B2B reps who want a full call, not just a pitch.

CloserCoach. The listing covers sales calls, elevator pitches and negotiations, with AI feedback on tone, persuasion and objection handling, scorecards, and a "Ridealong" mode for in-person selling. It names SDRs, closers, real estate agents, mortgage officers and insurance brokers. Monthly plans were listed at $13.99 to $29.99. Best for: high-ticket and consumer sales.

Speaking Coach (ours). Use the "Pitch your big idea" scene, or describe your buyer ("a CFO who thinks we're too expensive") and the voice partner plays them, following up on what you actually said. Pick Gentle, Realistic or Tough. Afterwards: your pace, filler words, long pauses and tips that quote your words, then 90 seconds to retry the moment you lost them. It also covers what surrounds a pitch: "Handle a rude customer", small talk before the call, presenting from slides. It isn't a sales-methodology tool: no MEDDPICC scorecards or CRM. iPhone and Android, 20 languages, subscription.

Orai. Its listing includes sales pitches among its scenarios and measures filler words, pace, clarity, energy, confidence and conciseness. Nobody objects, but if your pitch sounds flat or rushed, this is cheap, focused practice.

ELSA Speak. If you sell in English as a second language, its listing includes a Sales coach alongside pronunciation feedback.

For sales teams

Second Nature. Describes itself as a "virtual pitch partner" with video avatars for discovery calls, objection handling and cold calls, used by sales, enablement and support teams. Pricing is through sales, but its home page offers free role plays for cold calling, discovery and objection handling after you enter your details. Worth a look even if you're on your own.

Hyperbound. AI roleplays "built around your sales process", plus scoring of real calls. Scorecards explain each score. Its home page offers a trial "in 30 seconds, with no credit card". Best for: SDR teams drilling cold calls.

PitchMonster. AI buyers by phone or face to face, debriefed by a voice AI coach, scored against your own scorecards or templates like MEDDPICC. Its page says sessions run in 29 languages. Per-seat pricing on 12-month contracts, via a demo.

Yoodli. Its home page shows "a discovery call with a skeptical VP who has heard every pitch", scored on discovery questions, objection handling and next steps. Aimed mostly at sales readiness and training teams, but individuals can start with 5 free lifetime sessions.

What to rehearse, in order

  1. The first 30 seconds. Who you help, the problem, why now. Say it out loud until you don't need notes. Our elevator pitch guide has a template.
  2. Three objections. Price, timing, "we already use X". Write a one-sentence answer to each, then practice them out of order.
  3. The question you hope they won't ask. Rehearse that one twice as often.
  4. The ask. End with a specific next step ("Can we book 20 minutes with your ops lead next Tuesday?"), not "Any questions?".
  5. The full call at the tough setting. Then once more, gentle, the day before.

Questions people ask

What is the best app to practice a sales pitch on my own?

As of November 2026, SalesDojo and CloserCoach are phone apps built for sales calls with AI buyers. Speaking Coach also plays any buyer you describe and covers presentations, interviews and customer conversations in 20 languages. Team platforms like Second Nature and Hyperbound sell mainly to companies.

Is there a free way to practice a sales pitch with AI?

Second Nature's home page offers free role plays for cold calls, discovery and objections after you enter your details, Hyperbound offers a trial without a card, and Yoodli gives 5 free lifetime sessions. The free voice review on this site checks 2 minutes of your pitch for pace, fillers and pauses, and quotes one sentence to rewrite.

Can AI practice objection handling with me?

Yes. SalesDojo, CloserCoach, Second Nature, PitchMonster and Yoodli all name objection handling, and Speaking Coach's partner pushes back if you tell it what the buyer cares about. Ask for the objection you fear most, every time.

How long should a sales pitch be?

For a first call, your opening pitch should fit in about 30 to 60 seconds; at a typical 130 to 150 words a minute that's roughly 65 to 150 words. Then let the buyer talk.

Are AI sales roleplays realistic?

Realistic enough for repetitions: rehearsing objections until your answer is automatic. A real buyer is less predictable, so treat AI as the warm-up and record your real calls, with permission, for review.
