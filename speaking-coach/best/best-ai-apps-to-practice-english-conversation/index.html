Two different jobs called "English conversation practice"

People who search for an AI English conversation app usually want one of two things, and the best app is different for each:

  1. Learning English. You want vocabulary, grammar corrections and pronunciation help while you talk. You want lessons that get harder. That is a language course with an AI tutor.
  2. Using the English you have. You can already follow a conversation, but your mind goes blank when a stranger says hi, an interviewer asks "Why?", or a teacher calls on you. That is practice for a situation, not a lesson.

Most roundups mix these together. We split them below. We make Speaking Coach, which belongs in the second group; for every other app we opened its own site or App Store page in November 2026 and state only what we saw there. Prices are from the US App Store and vary by country.

Learning English with an AI tutor

Praktika. Its App Store listing describes AI tutors with different personalities and accents (American, British, Latin American and more) who listen, respond and correct you in real time, with explanations in your own language for beginners. It lists 1,000+ lessons for travel, careers, job interviews, everyday conversation, slang and IELTS/TOEFL prep, role-play scenarios, and lessons built from your own uploads such as a resume or PDF. Its site lets you set the tutor's tone to Soft, Balanced or Strict. Best for: beginners and intermediates who want a course that feels like a conversation.

Speak. Speak's site describes a Learn, Practice, Apply method: lessons teach phrases, drills repeat them, then you hold back-and-forth conversations with the Speak Tutor AI. Real-time feedback on pronunciation and phrasing is "a core part of the Speak experience". Non-English speakers can use it to practice spoken English. A 7-day free trial, then a paid plan. Best for: learners who like structure and steady progress.

Loora. Loora's site describes an English-only AI speaking partner for general topics and work scenarios like job interviews and business meetings, with real-time feedback on pronunciation, grammar and accent. iPhone and Android. Best for: intermediate speakers who mostly want to talk, a lot, about anything.

Speaking tests and pronunciation

ELSA Speak. The App Store listing describes a Speech Analyzer with detailed pronunciation feedback and a speaking score mapped to IELTS, TOEFL, CEFR and TOEIC, plus AI role-plays and coaches for interviews, sales, negotiation and presentations. Exam prep needs Premium. Best for: when pronunciation is the main thing holding you back.

Stimuler. Built around the IELTS Speaking test: a 60-second speech gets feedback on pronunciation, fluency and vocabulary with an estimated band score, plus filler words, pace and pauses. Premium adds full-length mock tests with an AI interviewer. Best for: IELTS candidates on a budget.

Using the English you already have

Speaking Coach (ours). An AI voice partner plays the other side of a real situation: an interviewer who reads your CV, an audience asking about your slides, a classmate, a coworker at the coffee machine, a first date. It follows up on what you actually said. Afterwards you see your pace, filler words, long pauses and tips that quote your words ("You said … / Try …"), then retry the moment that went wrong. It never rates your accent. It is not a language course: there are no grammar lessons or vocabulary drills. It works in 20 languages, so you can rehearse a scene in Filipino, Hindi or Spanish first, then in English. Subscription; see the App Store or Google Play for the price in your country.

ChatGPT voice. ChatGPT's voice page says you open it by tapping the voice icon, and mentions practicing a language. With a clear instruction ("Talk to me like a hotel receptionist, keep your answers short, correct only my biggest mistake at the end") it is a capable free partner. It won't measure your pace or fillers.

A free check. The free speech review on this site listens to up to 2 minutes of your English and shows measured pace, filler words and long pauses, with feedback that quotes your own words: one thing to keep, one change, one sentence rewritten more clearly. Two free reviews per network per day, no account; audio is transcribed on Speaking Coach's server and isn't kept. It does not score accent or pronunciation.

A human. Cambly's English page offers conversations with native-speaking tutors "24/7", with small groups from $12 a month and private lessons from $28 a month at the discounted rates shown. AI is cheaper per hour; a person notices things AI misses.

How to get more from any of them

  1. Speak more than you read. If the app shows text, try answering before you look at it.
  2. Pick real scenes. "Ordering coffee" is fine for week one. After that, practice what's coming up: your interview, your report, your small talk in English.
  3. Ask for one correction, not ten. Fix one thing per session.
  4. Repeat the same scene. The second run is where you notice progress.
  5. Don't chase an accent. Clear beats native-sounding. Our guide to speaking clearly with an accent explains what listeners actually need.

Questions people ask

What is the best AI app to practice English conversation?

It depends on your level. For learning, Praktika and Speak are the most complete courses with AI tutors as of November 2026. For using English you already know in interviews, class or social situations, Speaking Coach plays the other person and gives feedback on your delivery.

Is there a free AI app to practice speaking English?

ChatGPT's voice mode works as a free conversation partner if you tell it what role to play. The free voice review on this site gives measured feedback on two English answers a day. Stimuler's core app is free, and Praktika, Speak and ELSA offer trials or free starts.

Which app corrects my pronunciation?

ELSA Speak is built for it, with detailed pronunciation scores. Praktika, Speak and Loora also give pronunciation feedback while you talk. Speaking Coach doesn't rate pronunciation or accent; it focuses on pace, fillers, pauses and what you said.

Can AI replace an English tutor?

For volume, it comes close: you can talk at midnight, repeat a scene ten times and never feel judged. A human tutor is better at explaining why something sounds wrong and at noticing your habits over months. Many learners use both.

Which app is best for English job interviews?

Use a language app for vocabulary, then practice the interview itself with an interviewer who follows up. Speaking Coach reads your CV and the job post; Praktika and ELSA also offer interview lessons or coaches. See our list for non-native speakers.
