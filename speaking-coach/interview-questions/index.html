Common interview questions and how to answer them

By the Speaking Coach team at Sodhera Intelligence

What the interviewer is listening for, example answers for different levels of experience, the mistakes to avoid, and a way to practice each answer out loud.

  • Tell me about yourself: how to answer, with example answersAnswer 'Tell me about yourself' in 60–90 seconds with a present-past-future pitch. Example answers for graduates, career changers and experienced hires.
