What the ChatGPT app does

Speaking Coach in ChatGPT is a voice review. You speak; it listens to your recording and tells you how it sounded and what to change. It reviews your own voice: what it measures comes from your recording, not from text you type.

Each review gives you:

  • Your pace in words per minute.
  • Your filler words, such as "um", "like" and "you know".
  • Your pauses, including the long ones.
  • Feedback that quotes your spoken words, so you can see exactly which sentence to fix.
  • One change to try. Then you can try again and compare the second attempt with the first.

It is free. It doesn't rate your accent, pronunciation or personality, and it makes no medical assessment.

How to use it, step by step

  1. Open ChatGPT and type @Speaking Coach, or find Speaking Coach under Apps.
  2. Say what you are practicing in one line, for example the interview question or the speech.
  3. Optionally add a document as background: a PDF, Word, PowerPoint, text or Markdown file. Only its text is used, so a CV, a job post or your speech notes all work.
  4. Record or upload your voice. Answer the question or give the part of the speech you are working on. A minute or two is enough to see a pattern.
  5. Read the review. Start with the quoted line and the one change to try.
  6. Try again with that one change and compare the second attempt. Change one thing at a time; fixing everything at once usually fixes nothing.

Questions people ask

Is Speaking Coach in ChatGPT really free?

Yes. The voice review in ChatGPT is free, with two reviews per network connection per day, subject to shared capacity.

Can I use it in Spanish, Hindi or another language?

Not in ChatGPT, which works in English only. The phone app works in 20 languages, including Spanish, Hindi, Arabic and Japanese.

Will it judge my accent?

No. It doesn't assess accent, pronunciation or personality. It focuses on pace, filler words, pauses and the words you used.

Can I upload my CV or speech?

Yes, as an optional background document: PDF, Word, PowerPoint, text or Markdown. Only the text is used.

Is it the same as using ChatGPT's voice mode?

No. Speaking Coach measures your recording and quotes your own words back to you with one change to try. See Speaking Coach vs ChatGPT voice mode for a fuller comparison.
