How these pages are written

Every page is written for one question people actually ask, such as "How do I answer why should we hire you?" or "How long is a five-minute speech?". We follow a few rules:

  • The answer comes first. The first two sentences of every page answer the question.
  • No invented facts. We don't make up statistics, studies, reviews or testimonials. When we use a number from someone else, we link to where it came from.
  • Examples are examples. Sample answers and speeches show a structure. They are not real people's words, and you should replace the details with your own.
  • We say what we sell. Speaking Coach is our product. Where we compare it with other tools, we say so and point out where another tool fits better.
  • Dated and reviewed. Each page shows when it was last updated, and we review pages regularly.
