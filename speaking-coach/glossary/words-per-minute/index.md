# What is words per minute in speaking? Definition and examples

Words per minute (wpm) is how many words you say in one minute, the standard measure of speaking pace. Conversational English is commonly cited at about 120 to 160 wpm, and presentations often land around 130 to 150. To find yours, count the words in a recording and divide by its length in minutes.

## How to calculate it

Words per minute = words spoken ÷ minutes.

Worked examples:

- A 420-word talk that took 3 minutes: 420 ÷ 3 = **140 wpm**.
- A 90-second interview answer of 240 words: 240 ÷ 1.5 = **160 wpm**.
- A 5-minute speech of 550 words: 550 ÷ 5 = **110 wpm**.

To get the word count from a recording, use its captions or a transcript. Or use the [pace checker](/speaking-coach/tools/pace-checker/), which listens and calculates for you.

## Typical ranges

| Pace | Words per minute | Where it often fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slow | Under 110 | A eulogy, a toast, a line you want to land |
| Comfortable | About 110–170 | Conversation, interviews, most presentations |
| Fast | Over 170 | Excited storytelling; hard to follow for long |

The cut-offs above are the pace checker's settings, chosen around the commonly cited ranges, not a scientific line.

## Why the average isn't everything

Two people at 150 wpm can sound very different. One pauses after each point and speaks steadily in between; the other never stops. [Pauses](/speaking-coach/glossary/pause/) lower your average and make the same words easier to follow. Nerves often push pace up in the first minute, so check the start of a recording separately.

Pace also tells you how long a script will run: at 130 wpm, a 650-word speech takes about 5 minutes. ## Questions people ask

### What is a good speaking rate?

For most talks and interviews, somewhere around 120 to 160 words per minute, with pauses between ideas.

### How many words is a 5-minute speech?

About 600 to 750 words at 120 to 150 wpm.

### Is speaking fast bad?

Not always. It suits light stories. It's a problem when listeners miss key points or you run out of breath.

### How do I slow down?

Pause after every main point, shorten your sentences and breathe at full stops. See [how to slow down when speaking](/speaking-coach/guides/how-to-slow-down-when-speaking/).
