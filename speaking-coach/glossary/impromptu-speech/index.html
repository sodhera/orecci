Impromptu Speech: Meaning, Examples and Practice Topics

By the Speaking Coach team at Sodhera Intelligence · Updated November 17, 2026

An impromptu speech is a short talk you give with little or no preparation, usually with only seconds to think and no notes. Typical examples are being asked to "say a few words" at a dinner or leaving party, answering a surprise question in a meeting, or speaking on a topic you draw in class. It tests whether you can organise your thoughts quickly and still sound clear.

What impromptu means

"Impromptu" means done without planning. The word comes, through French, from the Latin in promptu: ready, at hand. An impromptu speech usually lasts one to two minutes, and you learn the topic just before you speak. You'll meet it in three settings:

  • Everyday life: any moment someone says "Can you say a few words?"
  • Speaking clubs: Toastmasters calls it Table Topics. Members are called on at random to answer an impromptu question or topic, in one to two minutes.
  • Speech competitions: in the National Speech & Debate Association's Impromptu event, students get seven minutes in total to prepare and deliver a speech, without notes.

Example impromptu speeches

These are made-up examples, each about 30 to 45 seconds out loud.

In class: "Something you changed your mind about"

"I used to think reading on a phone didn't count. Then last winter I had a long commute and no space for books, so I read four novels on my phone. I remembered them as well as any paper book. So now I think what counts is that you read, not what you read on."

Shape: point, reason, example, point, the PREP method.

At work: "Can you give us a quick update?"

"Short version: we're on track. Testing finished on Friday and we found two small bugs, both fixed. The one risk is the payment page, which needs sign-off from finance by Wednesday. I'll chase that today."

Shape: headline, what happened, what's next.

At a dinner: "Say something for the host"

"I just want to thank Mei. She's cooked for twelve people tonight, and she still found time to make a separate dish for me because I don't eat meat. That's Mei. To Mei."

Shape: thanks, one specific detail, toast.

Interviews

  • Sell me this pen
  • Teach me something in two minutes
  • What would you change about our product?
  • Describe your hometown to someone who has never been
  • What's a decision you'd make differently?

How to practise impromptu speaking

  1. Write topics on slips of paper, or use the list above.
  2. Draw one and give yourself ten seconds to choose one point.
  3. Speak for one minute out loud, with a timer.
  4. Finish with a planned last line, then stop.
  5. Record it and listen once: did you open cleanly, and did you end on purpose?

Questions people ask

What is an impromptu speech?

A short speech given with little or no preparation, usually without notes. You learn the topic just before you speak.

What is an example of an impromptu speech?

Being asked to say a few words at a colleague's leaving drinks, giving a surprise update in a meeting, or answering a topic drawn from a hat in class.

How long is an impromptu speech?

Usually one to two minutes. In class the limit is set in advance; at events, under a minute is often best.

Can you use notes in an impromptu speech?

Usually not. Sometimes you get a few seconds to jot two or three words, but mostly you speak without notes.

What is the difference between impromptu and extemporaneous?

Impromptu has little or no preparation. Extemporaneous is prepared and rehearsed but spoken from brief notes rather than a script.
