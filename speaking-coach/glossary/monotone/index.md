# What does monotone mean in speaking? Definition and example

A monotone voice stays at nearly the same pitch, pace and volume from start to finish, so every sentence sounds equally important and listeners struggle to tell what matters. It often happens when people read from a script, feel nervous, are tired or are concentrating hard on getting the words right.

## How it sounds in a sample line

Take this made-up update:

> "This quarter we lost two big clients, but we signed five new ones, and next quarter looks like our best yet."

**Said in a monotone:** the same pitch on "lost", "five" and "best yet", no pause after "clients", the same speed throughout. The bad news and the good news sound identical, so the listener has to work out which matters.

**Said with variety:** the voice drops a little on "lost two big clients", a short pause, it lifts on "five new ones", and it slows down on "our best yet". Now the sentence has a shape: problem, turn, payoff.

## Why it happens

| Cause | What's going on |
| --- | --- |
| Reading a script | Attention goes to the words on the page, not to meaning |
| Nerves | The voice tightens and its range narrows |
| Concentrating hard | Common when speaking a second language or reciting numbers |
| Tiredness or low energy | Less breath and effort, so less movement |
| Saying it for the hundredth time | Familiar material stops sounding interesting to you |
| Video calls | Talking to a screen with no reactions tends to flatten delivery |

Some people naturally speak with less pitch movement than others. That isn't a flaw; it just means pace, pauses and emphasis do more of the work.

## Monotone is not the same as calm

A calm voice can still vary: small drops for serious points, small lifts for new ones, pauses before the key word. Monotone means *no* variation, not low energy. ## Questions people ask

### What is an example of a monotone voice?

Someone reading a list of announcements at one pitch and speed, with no pauses, so nothing stands out.

### Is speaking in a monotone bad?

It isn't wrong, but it makes it harder for listeners to stay focused and spot your key points, especially in longer talks.

### Why do I sound monotone on recordings?

Nerves and reading from notes are common causes. Recordings also lack the faces and reactions that usually liven up how you talk.

### How do I know if I'm monotone?

[Record yourself](/speaking-coach/guides/record-yourself-speaking/) for a minute and listen for whether your most important sentence sounds any different from the rest.
