# Mock Interview: Meaning, How It Works and Example Questions

A mock interview is a practice interview that copies a real one: someone plays the interviewer, asks the questions you're likely to get, follows up on your answers and gives you feedback afterwards. It matters because answers that sound fine in your head often run long or go vague once you say them out loud under pressure, and a mock interview shows you that before it counts.

## Meaning

"Mock" here means practice or imitation, not mockery. It is also called a practice interview. What makes it a mock interview, rather than reading a list of questions, is that you answer out loud, to someone who reacts, in the same format as the real thing.

## Types of mock interview

| Type | What you practise | Example question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General | Your story and motivation | "Why do you want this job?" |
| Behavioral | Real examples from your past | "Tell me about a time you missed a deadline." |
| [Situational](/speaking-coach/glossary/situational-interview/) | Judgement in a made-up scenario | "What would you do if two deadlines clashed?" |
| Technical | Skills and explaining your thinking | "Walk me through how you'd find this bug." |
| Case | A business problem, solved out loud | "Why are this shop's profits falling?" |

You can also rehearse the setting: a panel of several interviewers, or a video or phone call. Most real interviews mix the first three types, so a general mock interview usually does too.

## How a mock interview works

1. **Pick the real target.** Share the job post and your CV with your interviewer.
2. **Prepare questions:** common, behavioral and role-specific.
3. **Run it in real conditions.** Same format (video, phone or in person), same length, no stopping to restart.
4. **Expect follow-ups** such as "What exactly did you do?" That is where answers get tested.
5. **Get feedback** straight after. Did each first sentence answer the question? Which answer ran longest? Where did you go vague, or say "we" when they wanted "I"?
6. **Redo the weakest answer** at once, so you leave with a better version.

## Example mock interview questions

Examples for a 20–30 minute run. Pick six to eight.

| Question | What a good answer does |
| --- | --- |
| "[Tell me about yourself](/speaking-coach/interview-questions/tell-me-about-yourself/)." | Present, past, why this job, in 60–90 seconds |
| "Why do you want this job?" | Names something specific to this role, not any job |
| "What are your strengths?" | One strength, proved with one example |
| "What is your greatest weakness?" | A real one, plus what you're doing about it |
| "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a colleague." | One story: your actions and the result |
| "Tell me about a time you made a mistake." | Owns it, then shows what changed |
| "What would you do if a customer demanded a refund you couldn't give?" | Steps in order, with reasons |
| "Walk me through a project you're proud of." | Your part, not just the team's |
| "How do you handle pressure?" | A real moment, not "I work well under pressure" |
| "Why should we hire you?" | What the job needs, proof, and what you'd bring |
| "Do you have any questions for us?" | One or two real questions about the work |

Your interviewer should add a follow-up to each, such as "How did you measure that?"

## Mock interview vs. practising alone vs. the real thing

| | Practising alone | Mock interview | Real interview |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Questions | You choose them | Partly unknown | Unknown |
| Follow-ups | None | Yes | Yes |
| Feedback | Only what you notice on a recording | Straight after | Rarely |
| Stakes | None | Low | High |

## Common mistakes

- Stopping to restart after a stumble. On the day you can't, so practise carrying on.
- Practising only the questions you like.
- Reading from notes you won't have on the day.
- Choosing someone who will only say "That was great."

## Questions people ask

### What is the purpose of a mock interview?

To practise your answers out loud under realistic pressure, with follow-ups, and get feedback while there's time to improve.

### How long is a mock interview?

Match the real interview if you can. A first run of 20 to 30 minutes with six to eight questions, plus 10 minutes of feedback, is enough.

### How many mock interviews should I do?

Two or three before an important interview is common. Leave time between them to work on the feedback.

### Can I do a mock interview alone?

You can record yourself answering a list of questions, but you miss the follow-ups. A friend or an AI partner adds those.

### Are mock interviews worth it?

Yes. They make you speak your answers to someone who reacts, before the day that counts.
