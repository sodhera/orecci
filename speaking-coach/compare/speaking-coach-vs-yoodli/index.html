Speaking Coach vs Yoodli: Which AI Speech Coach Should You Use?

By the Speaking Coach team at Sodhera Intelligence · Updated October 22, 2026

Pick Yoodli if you practice at a computer, want feedback tied to real meetings, or your company is rolling out roleplay training; its individual plans start free with 5 sessions. Pick Speaking Coach if you want to practice on your phone with a voice partner who plays the other side (an interviewer who has read your CV, an audience, a stranger) in any of 20 languages. We make Speaking Coach, so we've tried to be specific about where Yoodli is better.

The difference in one line each

  • Yoodli, as of October 2026, calls itself "The AI Platform for Human Skills": AI roleplays, scoring, coaching and dashboards, sold mostly to companies, with individual plans on the web.
  • Speaking Coach (ours): practice interviews, speeches and small talk out loud, with an AI partner, in 20 languages, on iPhone and Android.

Both use AI that talks back. The real differences are where you practice, who it's built for, and what happens around the session.

When Yoodli is the better choice

Be honest with yourself about these:

  • Your employer is paying. Yoodli's Business and Enterprise plans include custom scenarios, rubrics, analytics and integrations a training team needs. Speaking Coach has none of that.
  • You want feedback on real meetings, not just practice. Yoodli's public speaking page describes a coach that learns your role and sends a weekly digest on how you came across in real meetings. Speaking Coach only covers practice.
  • You practice at a desk. If your rehearsals happen on a laptop with your slides open, a web app fits.
  • You want a cheap annual plan. Yoodli lists Pro at $8 a month billed annually, and a free start.
  • Sales roleplays. Yoodli's scoring on things like discovery questions and objection handling is aimed squarely at sales teams.

When Speaking Coach is the better choice

  • You practice on your phone. On a walk, in the car before an interview, in a hotel room the night before a talk.
  • You're preparing for a job interview. You add your CV and the job post; the interviewer asks about your real experience and follows up. There's a 5-session plan for your interview day with a reminder the day before.
  • You practice in another language. Screens, voice partner and feedback work in 20 languages, including Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Spanish and Portuguese.
  • It's personal, not work. A first date, a flatmate conversation, an apology, a toast.
  • You get nervous. You can say "I need a moment" to pause or "Help me" for a sentence to start from, and choose Gentle, Realistic or Tough pressure.
  • You want to redo the bad moment. "Retry this moment" gives you 90 seconds on the exact part you fumbled, then shows what changed.

Neither tool should judge who you are. Speaking Coach never rates your accent or personality; see how we give feedback.

A quick test before you pay

Run the same task in both: a two-minute answer to tell me about yourself, or the opening of a talk.

  1. Did the partner react to what you said, or move to a stock next question?
  2. Is the feedback something you can act on in the next attempt?
  3. Would you actually open it tomorrow, where you practice?

The tool you'll use five times this week beats the better-featured one you open once. Yoodli's free Starter plan and Speaking Coach's free review in ChatGPT both let you try without committing.

Questions people ask

Is Yoodli free?

As of October 2026, its pricing page lists a free Starter plan with 5 lifetime sessions. Pro and Advanced are paid and billed annually.

Is Speaking Coach free?

The phone app is a subscription; new subscribers may get a free trial. The Speaking Coach app inside ChatGPT is free, in English, with two reviews per network connection per day.

Which is better for presentations?

Both work. Yoodli suits desk rehearsal with simulated Q&A and real-meeting feedback. Speaking Coach lets you swipe through your PDF slides on your phone while you speak, then answer audience questions.

Which is better for job interviews?

Speaking Coach is built around interviews that use your CV and job post, with follow-ups and an interview-day plan. Yoodli's pages we saw focus more on presentations and sales roleplays.

Can I use both?

Yes. Some people use Yoodli at work, where it's provided, and a phone app for personal practice and interviews.
