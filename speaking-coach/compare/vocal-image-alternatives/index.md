# Vocal Image Alternatives: Voice Training or Speaking Practice?

First decide which problem you have. If it's your voice itself (monotone, thin, shaky, unclear), you need voice training: Speeko, a human voice coach, or Vocal Image itself. If your voice is fine but you blank, ramble or rush when it matters, you need speaking practice: a partner who talks back, like Speaking Coach, Yoodli or, for interviews, Huru. It's easy to buy a voice app when the real problem is the second one.

## Two different problems

**Voice training** works on the instrument: pitch, tone, resonance, breath, articulation. The question it answers is "How does my voice sound?"

**Speaking practice** works on what you do with it under pressure: finding words, structuring an answer, pausing instead of saying "um", staying calm when someone pushes back. The question it answers is "What happens when I have to talk?"

A quick test: record yourself reading a paragraph you know well, then answering a question you didn't expect. If the reading sounds fine and the answer falls apart, your problem is speaking practice, not your voice.

## If you have both

The two are often linked: the voice tightens *because* you're nervous. Work on both, in this order:

1. **Two minutes of breathing and warm-up** before practice (any voice app or our [breathing guide](/speaking-coach/guides/how-to-breathe-while-speaking/)).
2. **Ten minutes of real speaking practice** on the situation you're dreading.
3. **Listen back once** for one voice issue and one content issue. Fix one of each next time.

## Questions people ask

### Is Vocal Image good for public speaking?

It helps the voice side: pitch, tone, articulation and breathing, plus some roleplay. If your trouble is what to say under pressure, add practice with a partner who talks back.

### Is there a free alternative to Vocal Image?

Vocal Image offers free voice tests. For speaking practice, Huru is free for interviews, and our free [speech review](/speaking-coach/tools/free-speech-review/) shows pace, fillers and pauses in your browser.

### Does Speaking Coach train my voice?

No. It doesn't do pitch, resonance or accent training. It's for practicing conversations, interviews and talks, with feedback on pace, fillers, pauses and what you said.

### Can an app fix a hoarse or painful voice?

No. Persistent hoarseness, pain or voice loss is a reason to see a doctor or a speech-language therapist, not to download an app.

### Which is better for job interviews?

A speaking practice tool with follow-up questions, such as Speaking Coach or Huru. Voice training helps you sound steadier, but interviews turn more on what you say and how calmly you say it.
