Speak in one paragraph

Speak is an AI language app built around a three-step method its site calls Learn, Practice, Apply: lessons teach the phrases native speakers use, drills repeat them until they're automatic, and then you hold back-and-forth conversations with the Speak Tutor AI. Real-time feedback on pronunciation and phrasing runs through all of it. As of November 2026, English speakers can learn Spanish, French, Korean, Japanese, Italian and Mandarin, non-English speakers can practice English, and other languages are on a waitlist. There is a 7-day free trial, then Premium or Premium Plus, which adds lessons built from your mistakes and a lesson creator. US App Store prices included $17.99 a month or $83.99 a year for Premium and $39.99 a month or $164.99 a year for Premium Plus; the site says prices vary by region.

It's a polished app. People look elsewhere for four reasons: the price after the trial, a language Speak doesn't teach yet, wanting less lesson and more talk, or wanting to use the language somewhere specific. We make Speaking Coach, one of the options below. We opened each alternative's own site or App Store page in November 2026 and state only what we saw.

The alternatives, briefly

Praktika. The nearest match. Its listing describes AI tutors with different accents who correct you in real time, explanations in your native language, role-play scenarios, IELTS/TOEFL prep and lessons built from your uploads. It names 13 languages to learn, including German, Portuguese, Arabic, Russian, Polish and Dutch, which Speak's site doesn't list for English speakers. Prices on the US App Store were lower than Speak's. Our Praktika alternatives page compares it further.

Loora. English only, and mostly talk: personalized conversations on general topics plus work scenarios like job interviews and business meetings, with real-time feedback on pronunciation, grammar and accent. iPhone and Android. Best for: English learners who find lessons slow and just want to speak.

ELSA Speak. Strongest on the Practice step for English: a Speech Analyzer that shows exactly what you mispronounced, with a speaking score mapped to IELTS, TOEFL, CEFR and TOEIC, plus AI role-plays. Best for: when people understand your grammar but not your words.

Speaking Coach (ours). Not a course. It skips Learn and Practice and goes straight to a real situation: an AI voice partner plays an interviewer who has read your CV, an audience asking about your slides, a stranger at a party, a manager you're asking for a raise. It follows up on what you actually said. Afterwards you see pace, filler words, long pauses and tips that quote your words, and you can retry the moment that went wrong. It works in 20 languages, including Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Italian and Chinese (Simplified), so it pairs well with Speak once you can hold a basic conversation. It never rates your accent. Subscription; see the App Store or Google Play for the price in your country.

Cambly. Human tutors, available "24/7" per its English page, with small groups from $12 a month and private lessons from $28 a month at the discounted rates shown. Best for: people who want a real reaction, not a model's.

Free options. ChatGPT's voice page mentions practicing a language; tell it your level and the role you want it to play. For English, the free speech review on this site measures your pace, filler words and long pauses over up to 2 minutes and quotes your words back: one thing to keep, one change, and one sentence rewritten more clearly. Two free reviews per network per day, no account or install, and the audio isn't kept.

Where Speak is still the better pick

  • You want one app that takes you from beginner to conversational in Spanish, French, Korean, Japanese, Italian or Mandarin, with a clear path.
  • You like lessons built from your own mistakes. That's Premium Plus.
  • Your employer is paying. Speak for Business adds workplace courses and an admin portal.

A two-app plan that works

Speak, Praktika and Loora teach you the words. They don't put you in front of an interviewer or a room. If something specific is coming up, such as a job interview in English, a presentation at university or your first language exchange, keep your course app for daily lessons and add two or three rehearsals of that exact situation in the week before it.

  1. Weeks 1 to 8: daily lessons in your course app, 15 minutes.
  2. From week 4: once a week, a five-minute conversation with no lesson attached: ChatGPT voice, Loora or a tutor.
  3. The week before the real thing: rehearse that scene three times, with a partner who follows up.

Questions people ask

What is the best alternative to the Speak app?

As of November 2026, Praktika is the closest alternative: AI tutors, lessons and role-play, more languages to learn, and lower US App Store prices. If you want conversation more than lessons, try Loora for English.

Is there a free version of Speak?

Speak's site says it offers a 7-day free trial, after which a paid subscription is required. For free practice, ChatGPT's voice mode works as a conversation partner, and the free voice review on this site checks two English answers a day.

Does Speak teach German or Portuguese?

Not to English speakers, as of November 2026: its site lists Spanish, French, Korean, Japanese, Italian and Mandarin, with other languages on a waitlist. Praktika's listing includes German and Portuguese.

Is Speak good for business English?

Speak for Business offers role-play and workplace-focused courses for companies. For a specific interview or presentation, Speaking Coach lets you rehearse that exact situation with a partner who follows up.

Can I use Speak and Speaking Coach together?

Yes, and they don't overlap much. Speak teaches the language; Speaking Coach is for using it in a real conversation, with feedback on how you sounded rather than on grammar.
