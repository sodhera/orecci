Speaking Coach vs Exponent: Which Do You Need for Your Interview?

By the Speaking Coach team at Sodhera Intelligence · Updated October 22, 2026

They mostly do different jobs. Speaking Coach (our app) trains how you say it: a voice interviewer that reads your CV and follows up, for behavioral and 'tell me about yourself' answers. Where Aced is the right tool

  • You need to learn the content. No amount of speaking practice will teach you how to design a rate limiter or structure a product-metrics answer. A course and real questions will.
  • You want a human to interview you, for free. Aced's practice page says you're matched with a peer preparing for similar interviews; each of you interviews the other for about 30 minutes over a live video call. Free users get monthly practice credits.
  • You want an experienced coach. Aced sells 60-minute coaching sessions with senior tech coaches; its page says most people book three to five.

Limits worth knowing: its practice page says peer mocks don't work on mobile, and transcripts and AI feedback on peer interviews need a paid membership.

Where Speaking Coach is the right tool

Technical candidates often know their stories and still lose the behavioral round. They tell them out of order, give the background for 90 seconds, or say "we" so often the interviewer can't tell what they did. That's a delivery problem, and you fix it by saying the answer many times to someone who interrupts.

Speaking Coach does that part. Add your CV and the job post, and the interviewer asks about your actual projects, then follows up: "What did you decide?", "What would you do differently?" Afterwards you see your pace, filler words and long pauses, and tips quoting your words. You get a 5-session plan for your interview day and a reminder the day before. You can practice any time, on your phone, without booking a partner.

Questions people ask

Is Exponent the same as Aced?

Yes. As of October 2026, tryexponent.com redirects to aced.io and the site calls itself "Aced (formerly Exponent)".

Are Aced's peer mock interviews free?

Its practice page says free users get practice credits every month, and members get unlimited access. Transcripts and AI feedback need a paid membership.

Can Speaking Coach help with system design interviews?

Not with the design itself: it won't tell you whether your architecture is right. It can help you practice explaining a design out loud clearly and handling interruptions.

Which should I use for a product manager interview?

Both, for different parts. Aced for frameworks, cases and real PM questions; Speaking Coach to rehearse your product stories and behavioral answers out loud. Our product manager interview questions page lists common ones.

Do I need paid coaching?

Not always. Free peer mocks plus daily spoken practice cover most needs. A paid human session is worth it near the end for senior roles, where someone who has hired at that level can calibrate you.
