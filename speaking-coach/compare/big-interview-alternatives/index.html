Big Interview Alternatives: Which Mock Interview Tool Is Better?

By the Speaking Coach team at Sodhera Intelligence · Updated October 22, 2026

Big Interview is strong on lessons: video courses, an answer builder and practice questions with AI feedback. If you want an interviewer who reacts to your answers, try Huru (free) or Speaking Coach (a voice partner that follows up). If you want free human practice, use Aced's peer mock interviews. If you want lessons and recorded practice at a one-time price, Big Interview itself is still a good choice.

What Big Interview is good at

As of October 2026, Big Interview's home page describes "170 video lessons with strategies, examples, and practice sets", a practice tool built on video interview questions, AI feedback on communication, body language and content, an answer builder, and a resume builder. It sells to individuals and to universities and workforce agencies. Prices shown were $39 for one month, $99 for three months and $299 for lifetime access, with a 30-day money-back guarantee.

That's a solid package if you want to learn what interviewers look for and then practice set questions on camera. Because it sells to university career centers, students should ask theirs whether they already have access before paying.

What it's not built around is a conversation. You answer a question; you don't get a second question that grows out of your first answer. That follow-up is where many real interviews get hard: "What did you do, specifically?" "Why that approach?" We make Speaking Coach, one of the tools that does this, so weigh our view accordingly.

The interviewers that react

Huru. Its site says the live AI interviewer adapts to your answers and asks follow-ups. You choose an interviewer, a pressure level (Supportive, Standard, Demanding) and a length from 5 to 20 minutes, with audio or video. Each answer gets a score and a rewritten version; video sessions add eye contact and posture notes. It says all of this is free for job seekers.

Speaking Coach (ours). You add your CV, the job post or your notes. The interviewer, a voice on your phone, asks about your real experience and follows up on what you actually said, at Gentle, Realistic or Tough pressure. Stuck? Say "Help me" for a sentence to start from. Afterwards you get pace, filler words, long pauses, how quickly you started, and tips that quote your words ("You said … / Try …"). Then you can retry the moment you fumbled for 90 seconds. It works in 20 languages. There's also a 5-session plan for your interview day with a reminder the day before.

Gemini Live. Google's own interview tips page suggests using Gemini Live, in the Gemini mobile app, to simulate a conversational interview. It's a general assistant, so you set the role yourself and won't get measured pace or filler counts.

When a human is worth paying for

AI partners give you repetitions. A human who has interviewed for your role gives you judgment. Aced's peer mocks are free with monthly credits and pair you with another candidate; you take turns. For engineering roles, interviewing.io's FAQ says sessions start at $179 and offers a full refund if you're unhappy. One paid human mock, after a week of AI practice, is a sensible budget plan.

A one-week plan that mixes tools

  1. Day 1: Watch the lessons you need (Big Interview, or our free interview questions pages) and draft three stories using the STAR method.
  2. Days 2 to 5: One 10-minute mock a day with an interviewer that follows up. Fix one thing each time.
  3. Day 6: One human mock, peer or paid.
  4. Day 7: A short warm-up only. Say your opening answer twice and stop.

Questions people ask

Is Big Interview worth it?

It is if you want structured video lessons plus practice on set questions, and its one-time pricing and 30-day money-back guarantee lower the risk. If you mainly want back-and-forth practice, a tool with follow-up questions may suit you better.

Is there a free alternative to Big Interview?

Yes. Huru says its AI interview practice is free for job seekers, Aced's peer mocks have free monthly credits, and if you're a student, your career center may already give you Big Interview access.

Which alternative gives feedback on body language?

Big Interview's site lists body-language feedback, and Huru's video mode covers eye contact, posture and expressions. Speaking Coach is voice only and does not assess body language.

Do AI interviewers ask real follow-up questions?

Some do. Huru and Speaking Coach both describe interviewers that respond to what you said rather than reading the next question from a list. Try one session and see if the follow-ups feel specific to your answer.

Can I practice for a non-English interview?

Speaking Coach works in 20 languages and Huru lists five. Big Interview's site, as we saw it, is in English.
