A Note on Encrypted Backup

Your journal is end-to-end encrypted. We cannot read your entries, and we cannot recover them for you — there is no reset we can perform on our side. If you are setting up a new device, restore from the same Apple ID so your key travels with you, or use your recovery phrase.

Subscriptions & Refunds

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID. To manage, cancel, or request a refund, use your Apple ID account settings on your device. Note that deleting your JournalBlock account does not cancel your subscription. We're happy to help with anything else by email.
