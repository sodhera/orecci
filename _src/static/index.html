How a practice works

  1. Pick the moment. A job interview, a toast, a presentation, asking for a raise, or a stranger who says hi.
  2. Talk it through. The AI partner listens and asks the follow-up a real person would. Say “Help me” if you get stuck.
  3. See how you sounded. Pace, fillers, long pauses, and tips that quote what you said, with “You said … / Try …”.
  4. Retry the moment. 90 seconds on the same moment, then see what changed.
