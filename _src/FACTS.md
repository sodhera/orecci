## What the phone app does (iPhone and Android)

- You practice out loud with an **AI voice partner who plays the other side**: an interviewer, an audience, a manager, a coworker, a customer, a date, or a stranger who says hi. The partner listens and **follows up on what you actually said**.
- **Mock interviews:** add your CV, the job post or your notes; the interviewer reads them and asks about your real experience. Classic questions: tell me about yourself, explain your experience with an example, think under pressure. A 5-session plan for your interview day, with a reminder the day before.
- **Presentations:** add your slides as a PDF, swipe through them like a clicker while you speak, then answer the questions your audience would ask.
- **Small talk and hard conversations:** a stranger says hi and you keep it going; meet someone new, ask for a raise, set a boundary.
- **Any situation:** describe the conversation and the partner plays the other side.
- **Pressure levels:** Gentle, Realistic or Tough. **"I need a moment"** pauses; **"Help me"** gives a sentence to start from.
- **Feedback after each session:** how you sounded (pace in words per minute, filler words, long pauses or freezes, how quickly you started), what to keep, and tips that quote something you actually said ("You said … / Try …"). **Retry this moment:** 90 seconds on the same moment, then see what changed.
- **Never rates your accent or your personality.**
- **Habit:** a daily 30-second speaking prompt, a streak, and a history of every session.
- **Languages (20):** English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Dutch, Polish, Swedish, Turkish, Russian, Ukrainian, Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified), Indonesian, Filipino, Vietnamese. Screens, voice partner and feedback work in all of them.
- **Privacy:** CV and documents stay on the phone; only their text goes to the AI partner during interview sessions.
- **Price:** a subscription, monthly or yearly. New subscribers may get a free trial. Do **not** state prices, discounts or trial lengths; say "see the App Store or Google Play for the price in your country". There is no free tier in the phone app.

## The free tools on orecci.com (built into these pages)

They run in the visitor's browser. Speech is turned into text by the browser's own speech recognition (in Chrome and Edge this is Google's or Microsoft's service; Safari uses Apple's). Speaking Coach's servers receive nothing. Where the browser has no speech recognition, the tools accept pasted text.

- **Speech checker** (used by the free speech review, interview answer review, small talk drill and conversation drill pages): speak, then see words per minute, filler words, long pauses and your transcript.
- **Filler word counter:** speak or paste text; counts fillers for the page's language.
- **Pace checker:** words per minute from speech, or from a word count and a time.
- **Speech length calculator:** words ↔ minutes at slow, average and fast pace.
- **Interview questions by role:** pick a role, get its practice questions.

These tools are not AI feedback. AI feedback (quoted tips, a partner who talks back) is in the ChatGPT app (English, free, limited) and the phone app (20 languages, subscription).
