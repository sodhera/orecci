## Who we are, and what we checked

We make Speaking Coach. Note that Vocal Image's App Store name also includes the words "Speaking Coach"; they are a different company. We read Vocal Image's home page and App Store page in October 2026, and our facts about it come only from those.

Both apps want you to speak better, but they start from different ends. **Vocal Image starts with the instrument**: pitch, clarity, pace, breathing, articulation, intonation and accent. **Speaking Coach starts with the situation**: an interviewer, a manager, a stranger at a party, and what you say back to them.

## Symptom to tool

Find the sentence that sounds most like you.

| What you notice | Likely cause | Better first tool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "My voice is monotone and flat" | Little pitch variation | Vocal Image: intonation practice matches pitch curves in real time |
| "I mumble and people ask me to repeat" | Articulation | Vocal Image: tongue twisters and diction drills with Clarity and Speed scores |
| "My voice shakes when I'm nervous" | Breath and nerves | Vocal Image: guided breathing; see also our guide on [voice shaking](/speaking-coach/guides/how-to-stop-voice-shaking/) |
| "I want to reduce my accent" | Pronunciation | Vocal Image: its listing includes accent reduction and English pronunciation. Speaking Coach never rates accents |
| "I ramble when someone asks me a question" | No structure under pressure | Speaking Coach: answer questions, see where you lost the point |
| "I go blank when an interviewer follows up" | Not practiced live | Speaking Coach: an interviewer that reads your CV and follows up |
| "Conversations die after two lines" | Few follow-up habits | Speaking Coach: "Keep a conversation going" and "Meet someone new" scenes |
| "I say um and like constantly" | Habit plus nerves | Either: both report filler words |
| "I talk too fast" | Pace | Either: Speaking Coach measures words per minute |
| "I can't say no or ask for a raise" | Hard conversation | Speaking Coach: "Say no", "Ask for a raise" scenes with pushback |

## What Vocal Image offers (October 2026)

Its App Store page calls it "an AI communication and soft skills coach". The listing and site describe:

- **Voice training** with instant voice analysis and warm-ups.
- **Articulation Trainer**: tongue twisters and diction drills with Clarity and Speed scores.
- **Intonation**: matching pitch curves in real time.
- **Breathing and calm** exercises.
- **Daily plans** with short coach videos, mini podcasts, and a library including accent reduction and English pronunciation.
- **AI roleplay**: "Practice a real conversation with an AI that plays the other person", such as interviews and salary talks, with feedback on filler words and phrasing.
- Free tests such as a voice test and an accent test.

The App Store page lists 10 languages and several subscription options in the US, from a $7.99 weekly plan to annual plans between $39.99 and $129.99. Which one you see may vary. It runs on iOS, Android and the web, per its site.

So Vocal Image is not only voice drills; it has conversation roleplay too. Its centre of gravity, though, is the voice.

## What Speaking Coach offers

A voice partner in 23 built-in scenes, from "Tell me about yourself" to "Deliver bad news" and "First date", or any situation you describe. You add your CV and the job post for interviews, or slides for a presentation. You choose Gentle, Realistic or Tough. Afterwards: pace in words per minute, filler words, long pauses, how quickly you started, and tips that quote what you said. Then **Retry this moment** for 90 seconds. It works in 20 languages and never rates your accent or personality.

What it doesn't have: vocal exercises, breathing drills, pitch training or accent work. If the sound of your voice is the issue, it isn't the right tool.

## Which to choose

- **Your voice works, but conversations go wrong:** Speaking Coach.
- **Your voice is the problem:** Vocal Image, or free techniques from our guides on [sounding less monotone](/speaking-coach/guides/how-to-stop-sounding-monotone/) and [projecting your voice](/speaking-coach/guides/how-to-project-your-voice/).
- **You want accent training:** Vocal Image. We deliberately don't do it.
- **Not sure:** record yourself answering "Tell me about yourself" for one minute and listen back. If you wince at how it *sounds*, start with voice training. If you wince at what you *said*, start with conversation practice.

## Questions people ask

### Is Vocal Image the same app as Speaking Coach?

No. Vocal Image's App Store name is "Vocal Image: Speaking Coach", but it is made by Vocal Image OU. Speaking Coach is made by Sodhera Intelligence.

### Does Vocal Image do mock interviews?

Its site describes AI roleplay for conversations such as interviews and salary talks. Speaking Coach's interview mode reads your CV and the job post and asks about your real experience.

### Which app helps with a monotone voice?

Vocal Image, which has intonation practice that matches pitch curves. Speaking Coach gives feedback on pace, fillers and pauses, but not pitch.

### Does Speaking Coach help with accent?

No. It never rates your accent. If you want accent or pronunciation training, choose a tool built for it.

### How much does Vocal Image cost?

As of October 2026 its US App Store page lists several plans, from a $7.99 weekly subscription to annual plans between $39.99 and $129.99. Check the store for the plan offered to you.
