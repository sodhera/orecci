## What Praktika is, as of November 2026

Praktika is an AI language tutor app. Its App Store listing describes AI tutors with distinct personalities and accents (American, British, Latin American and more) that listen, respond and correct you in real time, with explanations in your native language for beginners. It lists 1,000+ lessons covering travel, careers, job interviews, business, everyday conversation, slang and IELTS/TOEFL prep, plus role-play scenarios, flashcards and lessons built from your own uploads, such as a PDF or a resume. The listing names 13 languages you can learn, from English and Spanish to Japanese, Arabic and Dutch. Its site lets you set the tutor's tone to Soft, Balanced or Strict.

Prices on the US App Store included $9.99 for a month, $49.99 for three months and $119.99 for a year, with other offers listed; its home page compares "~$8/ month" with a private tutor. The iPhone app requires iOS 18 or later.

That's a lot for the price, and for many learners Praktika is the right app. People look for alternatives for a handful of specific reasons. We make Speaking Coach, one of the options below; for every app we opened its own site or store page in November 2026.

## The alternatives, briefly

**Speak.** The closest like-for-like swap. Speak's site describes lessons that teach phrases natives use, drills to make them automatic, then conversations with the Speak Tutor AI, with real-time feedback on pronunciation and phrasing. Non-English speakers can use it to practice spoken English. There's a 7-day free trial; US App Store prices included $17.99 a month and $83.99 a year for Premium, and $39.99 a month for Premium Plus. Best for: learners who want a clear path and don't mind paying more.

**Loora.** English only. Its site describes personalized conversations about anything from sports to your latest show, plus work scenarios like job interviews and business meetings, with real-time feedback on pronunciation, grammar and accent. Monthly prices on the US App Store ranged from $14.99 to $19.99. Best for: intermediate speakers who want volume over lessons.

**ELSA Speak.** Pronunciation first: a Speech Analyzer that shows what you missed and how to fix it, with a speaking score mapped to IELTS, TOEFL, CEFR and TOEIC. It also has AI role-plays and coaches for interviews, sales and presentations. 7-day trial. Our [ELSA Speak alternatives](/speaking-coach/compare/elsa-speak-alternatives/) page goes deeper.

**Stimuler.** Built around the IELTS Speaking test, with feedback on pronunciation, fluency, vocabulary, filler words, pace and pauses, and full-length mock tests with an AI interviewer in Premium. The listing says the core app is free. Best for: IELTS candidates.

**Speaking Coach (ours).** Different job from Praktika. There are no lessons: an AI voice partner plays the other side of a situation you're actually facing, such as an interviewer who has read your CV, an audience asking about your slides, a coworker at the coffee machine or a first date, and follows up on what you said. Afterwards you see your pace, filler words, long pauses and tips that quote your own words, then retry the moment that went wrong. It never rates your accent. It works in 20 languages, so you can rehearse in Filipino, Hindi, Indonesian or Vietnamese as well as English. Subscription; see the App Store or Google Play for the price in your country.

**Cambly.** Human tutors, "24/7". At the discounted rates shown on its English page, small groups were $12 a month and private lessons from $28 a month. Best for: learners who want someone to explain *why* a sentence sounds wrong.

**Free options.** ChatGPT's voice mode makes a decent conversation partner if you tell it the role and level. The [free speech review](/speaking-coach/tools/free-speech-review/) on this site listens to up to 2 minutes of English and gives measured pace, filler words and long pauses, with feedback quoting your words: one thing to keep, one change, one sentence rewritten more clearly. Two per network per day, no account; the audio isn't kept.

## Where Praktika is still the better pick

- **Complete beginners.** Explanations in your native language and graded lessons matter more than realism at the start.
- **Learning one of its 13 languages from English.** Speaking Coach isn't a language course, and Speak teaches fewer languages to English speakers.
- **Price.** Its yearly plan and "~$8/ month" positioning are below most alternatives here.
- **Lessons from your own material.** Uploading a resume or PDF to build a lesson is unusual.

## A sensible combination

Many learners use two tools: a course app for vocabulary and corrections, and a situation app for the moment that actually scares them. For example, Praktika or Speak four days a week, then a mock interview or a class presentation rehearsal before the real one. If small talk is the goal, our [English small talk guide](/speaking-coach/small-talk/small-talk-in-english/) has lines worth practicing in either.

## Questions people ask

### Is there a free alternative to Praktika?

As of November 2026, Stimuler's App Store page says its core app is free, and ChatGPT's voice mode is free with sign-up. The free voice review on this site gives two English reviews a day with no account. None is a full course like Praktika.

### Is Speak better than Praktika?

They're close. Speak has a clearer lesson structure and a 7-day trial; Praktika lists more languages to learn, explanations in your native language and lower US prices. Try both trials and keep the one you open without being reminded.

### Which Praktika alternative doesn't judge my accent?

Speaking Coach never rates your accent or personality. Loora's site says it helps "enhance your accent", and ELSA is built around pronunciation scores, so they're the opposite choice.

### Does Praktika help with job interviews?

Its listing includes job interview lessons and lets you upload a resume. If you want a mock interviewer who asks about your real experience and follows up, Speaking Coach reads your CV and the job post; see also our [AI apps for English conversation](/speaking-coach/best/best-ai-apps-to-practice-english-conversation/).

### Which app is best for Filipino speakers practicing English?

If you want lessons, Praktika's and Speak's app screens are offered in many languages, though Filipino isn't among those their App Store listings show. Speaking Coach works fully in Filipino and English, so you can rehearse a scene in Filipino first, then in English.
