## What Speeko offers, as of October 2026

Speeko's site describes an AI speech coach with real-time voice tracking and "simple, friendly alerts", analysis of pace, filler words, intonation, sentiment, talk time and word choice, and personalized exercises of about two minutes a day. Its toolkit includes digital notecards, interview prompts, vocal warm-ups and content from voice coach Roger Love. It also offers 1:1 work with a coach and links to a live coaching page. It runs on iOS, macOS, Android and the web.

Its subscriptions page listed $29.99 a month, an annual plan at $8.33 a month with a 7-day free trial, and $299.99 lifetime. The free tier offers basic insights.

People usually look elsewhere for one of four reasons. We make Speaking Coach, which fits some of them, and we say plainly where other tools fit better.

## Less course, more realistic practice

Exercises build skills one at a time. Real speaking is messier: someone asks a question you didn't plan for, or looks bored, or pushes back. If you've done the drills and still feel unready, you need practice that reacts.

**Speaking Coach (ours)** gives you an AI voice partner who plays the other side: your audience, an interviewer who has read your CV, a manager, a customer or a stranger at a party. It follows up on what you said. Afterwards you see your pace, fillers, long pauses and how quickly you started, with tips that quote your words, and you can retry the one moment that went wrong for 90 seconds. There's a daily 30-second speaking prompt if you like Speeko's daily-habit side.

**Yoodli** offers AI roleplays and audiences in the browser, with simulated Q&A after a presentation. It's strongest if you practice at a desk.

## A cheaper option

If you like Speeko's analysis and don't need the coaching content, **Orai** is the most direct, cheaper swap: its App Store listing describes feedback on fillers, pace, clarity, energy, confidence and conciseness, at $12.99 a month or $49.99 a year (English only, as of October 2026). For presentations, **PowerPoint Speaker Coach** is free in preview. For a single quick check, our free [pace checker](/speaking-coach/tools/pace-checker/) and [filler word counter](/speaking-coach/tools/filler-word-counter/) need no account.

## Interviews and small talk instead of speeches

Speeko's focus is public speaking. If your real dread is a job interview or walking up to strangers, practice that directly.

- **Interviews:** Huru (free AI interviewer with follow-ups) or Speaking Coach (uses your CV and the job post, with a 5-session plan for your interview day).
- **Small talk:** Speaking Coach's scenes include "Meet someone new", "Keep a conversation going", "Chat at the coffee machine", "Work the room" and "First date". Our guide to [networking events](/speaking-coach/small-talk/networking-event/) gives you lines to try.

## When to stay with Speeko

- You enjoy short guided exercises and vocal warm-ups.
- Real-time alerts while you speak help you more than a report afterwards.
- You value the voice coaching content and the option of a human coach.
- You want one app across phone, Mac and web.

## Questions people ask

### Is Speeko free?

It has a free tier with basic insights. As of October 2026, Pro was listed at $29.99 a month, $8.33 a month on the annual plan with a 7-day trial, or $299.99 lifetime.

### What is the cheapest Speeko alternative?

For presentations, PowerPoint Speaker Coach is free in preview. For a paid app with similar delivery metrics, Orai's App Store prices were lower as of October 2026.

### Which alternative has a partner who talks back?

Speaking Coach and Yoodli both use AI that responds to what you say. Huru does this for interviews and is free for job seekers.

### Does Speeko have live coaching?

Its site says you can work 1:1 with a coach and links to a live coaching page. Check that page for the current format and price.

### Can I practice small talk with Speeko?

Speeko's focus is public speaking. For small talk, use an app with conversation scenes, like Speaking Coach, or practice with real people using a guide like [how to keep a conversation going](/speaking-coach/small-talk/how-to-keep-a-conversation-going/).
