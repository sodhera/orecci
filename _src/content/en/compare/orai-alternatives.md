## What Orai does well

As of October 2026, Orai's site calls it a "friendly pocket coach" for a few minutes a day, and its App Store listing describes feedback on filler words, pace, clarity, energy, confidence and conciseness, with transcripts, short lessons, and scripted or freestyle practice for presentations, interviews, meetings and pitches. It runs on iOS, Android and the web. In-app prices listed on the App Store were $12.99 a month, $49.99 a year or $99.99 lifetime. The listing shows English as its language.

That's a good tool for one job: you speak alone, it measures. If you're looking elsewhere, it's usually for one of the reasons below. We make Speaking Coach, one of the alternatives, so read our take with that in mind.

## If you want a partner who talks back

Recording alone trains delivery. It doesn't train the moment someone interrupts with "But why would we pay for that?" Practice with a partner fixes that.

- **Speaking Coach (ours)** gives you an AI voice partner who plays the other side: an interviewer who has read your CV, an audience that asks about your slides, a manager, a customer or a stranger at a party. It follows up on what you actually said. You can pause with "I need a moment" or ask "Help me" for a sentence to start from. Afterwards: pace, fillers, long pauses and tips quoting your words, then a 90-second retry of the moment you fumbled.
- **Yoodli** runs roleplays and audience simulations in the browser, with a free start of 5 sessions.
- **Huru** is free for job seekers if your goal is interviews, with an AI interviewer that asks follow-ups.

## If you want feedback in another language

Orai's App Store listing shows English only. Speaking Coach's screens, voice partner and feedback work in 20 languages, including Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil) and Chinese (Simplified). Huru lists English, Spanish, Portuguese, German and French for its interviews.

## If you want presentation-specific practice

- **PowerPoint Speaker Coach** is free in preview, per Microsoft, and flags when you read your slides, plus pace, fillers and pitch. English only.
- **Speaking Coach** lets you add slides as a PDF, swipe through them like a clicker while you speak, then answer the questions your audience would ask.

## If you just want different drills

**Speeko** is the closest like-for-like swap: short daily exercises, vocal warm-ups, notecards and analysis of pace, fillers, intonation and word choice, on phone, Mac and web. **Vocal Image** leans toward the voice itself: pitch, tone, articulation and breathing.

## Keep using Orai if…

- you mainly read a script and want to sound smoother;
- filler counts and a clarity score motivate you;
- you like a low annual price and a short daily habit.

Adding one partner session a week to an Orai habit is often better than switching entirely. For the habit side, see [how to stop saying um](/speaking-coach/guides/stop-saying-um/), or check your fillers with our free [filler word counter](/speaking-coach/tools/filler-word-counter/).

## Questions people ask

### Is Orai free?

Orai is free to download. As of October 2026, its App Store listing shows paid plans at $12.99 a month, $49.99 a year or $99.99 lifetime.

### Does Orai work in other languages?

Its App Store listing shows English only, as of October 2026. For practice in other languages, Speaking Coach works in 20.

### What is the best Orai alternative for job interviews?

An app with an interviewer who follows up: Huru (free, per its site) or Speaking Coach, which uses your CV and the job post.

### What is the cheapest Orai alternative?

PowerPoint Speaker Coach is free in preview for presentations, and our browser-based [pace checker](/speaking-coach/tools/pace-checker/) is free for checking your speed.

### Can Orai help with presentations?

Yes, its listing includes presentation scenarios and scripted practice. It doesn't show your slides, so for slide-by-slide rehearsal use PowerPoint Speaker Coach or Speaking Coach.
