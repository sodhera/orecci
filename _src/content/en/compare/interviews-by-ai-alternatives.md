## What Interviews by AI does, as of November 2026

Interviews by AI is a web tool for answering interview questions one at a time. Its home page says you practice "out loud or in writing, with a timer", using questions generated from a job description you paste, common interview questions, or your own. Sample roles include Software Engineer, Product Manager, Data Analyst and UX/UI Designer. Each answer gets a score on structure, specificity, delivery and filler words, notes on what to fix and a rewritten sample answer, judged against STAR and other frameworks.

Its pricing page lists two plans. Basic is $0 with three practice questions a month, audio or text answers and instant AI feedback, no card needed. Pro is $9 a month for unlimited questions and resume upload, cancel anytime. Neither page mentions follow-up questions, a phone app or languages other than English.

So it's a tidy answer-grader. People look for alternatives because three questions a month runs out on day one, or because they want the part that actually makes interviews hard: the interviewer's next question. We make Speaking Coach, one of the options below. For every tool we opened its own site in November 2026 and state only what we saw.

## The alternatives, one by one

**Huru.** The obvious free switch. Huru's site says "Huru is free for everyone" and "Every feature is free for job seekers". The live AI interviewer "listens to what you say, asks follow-up questions and adapts", with three named interviewers, Supportive, Standard or Demanding pressure, and sessions of 5 to 20 minutes, by video or audio only. It works in English, Spanish, Portuguese, German and French, in a browser, on iOS and through a Chrome extension that turns a job post into an interview. We saw no native Android app listed. Best for: the most interview practice for $0.

**Speaking Coach (ours).** You add your CV, the job post or your notes, and an AI voice interviewer asks about your real experience, then follows up on what you said. Choose Gentle, Realistic or Tough; say "I need a moment" to pause or "Help me" for a sentence to start from. Afterwards you see your pace, filler words, long pauses, how quickly you started and tips that quote your words, then retry the moment that went wrong. There is a 5-session plan for your interview day with a reminder the day before. iPhone and Android, 20 languages; your CV stays on your phone and only its text goes to the AI during interview sessions. Subscription; see the App Store or Google Play for the price in your country.

**The free voice review on this site (ours).** Closest to what Interviews by AI's free plan does, with more of it. Answer an interview question out loud for up to 2 minutes on our English interview pages or the [free speech review](/speaking-coach/tools/free-speech-review/), and you get your measured pace, filler words and long pauses, plus feedback that quotes your words: one thing to keep, one change, and one sentence rewritten more clearly. Two free reviews per network per day, English only, no account, no install. Audio goes to Speaking Coach's server for transcription and isn't kept. It doesn't ask follow-ups.

**Big Interview.** A course with practice attached: 170 video lessons, an answer builder and a practice simulator with feedback on communication, body language and content. One month is $39, three months $99, lifetime $299, with a 30-day money-back guarantee. Its site quotes a school that gives students and alumni free access, so check with your career office first. Best for: people who want to be taught a method step by step.

**Yoodli.** Mostly sold to companies, but its Starter plan gives individuals 5 free lifetime roleplay sessions; Pro is $8 a month billed annually. Best for: a few web sessions if you also practice presentations.

**ChatGPT voice.** Free with sign-up. Paste the job post, ask it to interview you and to follow up when you're vague. It won't measure your pace or fillers, and you have to keep it in character. Our [ChatGPT voice mode comparison](/speaking-coach/compare/speaking-coach-vs-chatgpt-voice-mode/) has prompts that work.

**Language learners.** If English itself is the hurdle, Praktika's listing includes job interview lessons and resume uploads inside a full English course.

## Where Interviews by AI is still a good choice

- **You want to type.** Writing answers first, then speaking them, suits some people, and few voice tools allow text.
- **You want a rewritten model answer** for every question to study.
- **$9 a month, month to month,** is cheaper than most paid interview tools here.

## How to practice without running out of free questions

1. **Pick your five hardest questions.** Usually "Tell me about yourself", a failure, a conflict, "Why this job?" and one role-specific question.
2. **Draft each with the [STAR method](/speaking-coach/guides/star-method/)** in bullet points, not full sentences.
3. **Get two spoken reviews a day.** Fix the one change suggested, then review the same answer again tomorrow.
4. **Add follow-ups.** Once your first answers are solid, run a live mock with Huru or Speaking Coach, where the second question is the test.

## Questions people ask

### Is Interviews by AI free?

Partly. As of November 2026, its Basic plan is $0 with three practice questions a month; Pro is $9 a month for unlimited questions and resume upload.

### What's the best free alternative to Interviews by AI?

Huru, whose site says every feature is free for job seekers, including a live interviewer with follow-ups. For quick checks of single spoken answers, the free voice review on this site gives two a day without an account.

### Does Interviews by AI ask follow-up questions?

Its home and pricing pages don't mention follow-ups, as of November 2026. Huru and Speaking Coach both run live interviews that follow up on what you said.

### Is there a mock interview app that reads my resume?

Speaking Coach reads your CV, the job post or your notes on your phone. Interviews by AI uses your resume on its Pro plan, and Huru tailors questions to a job post you paste.

### Which alternative works in languages other than English?

Speaking Coach works in 20 languages and Huru in five. The free voice review on this site is English only, and Interviews by AI's pages don't mention other languages.
