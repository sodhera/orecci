## Who we are, and what we checked

We make Speaking Coach, so read this as a comparison written by one side. Everything we say about Speeko comes from its home page, its coaching page and its App Store page, all read in October 2026.

Both apps have AI feedback on pace and filler words, and both now let you practice conversations. The difference is in what each is built around. **Speeko is built around a training program**: lessons, courses, warm-ups and human coaches. **Speaking Coach is built around scenes**: you pick a situation, and a voice partner plays the person on the other side.

## Where Speeko is stronger

Speeko is the more complete *public speaking curriculum*. It tells you what to work on next, gives short daily exercises, and has expert content including material from voice coach Roger Love, per its site. Its coaching page offers a 30-minute 1:1 consultation for $49.99, with a personalized roadmap afterwards, and its App Store notes describe group Live Events with coaches. If you want someone to teach you delivery step by step, that structure is the point. It also runs on more devices, including Mac and the web.

## Where Speaking Coach is stronger

Most high-stakes speaking isn't a speech. It's an interviewer asking "Can you give me an example?", a manager saying "There's no budget this year", or someone at a party giving you a one-word answer. Speaking Coach puts you in those moments. Choose Gentle, Realistic or Tough pressure. If you freeze, say "I need a moment", or "Help me" for a sentence to start from. After the session, **Retry this moment** gives you 90 seconds on the line that went wrong. Our guide on how to [speak with confidence](/speaking-coach/guides/how-to-speak-with-confidence/) in everyday talk explains why this kind of rep matters.

## Can you use both?

Yes, and it's a reasonable split if you have both goals: Speeko for delivery skills and warm-ups, Speaking Coach for the conversations those skills are for. If budget means picking one, decide by the next real event in your calendar. If it's a talk, choose Speeko. If it's a conversation with someone who will answer back, choose Speaking Coach. Either way, our [30-day public speaking plan](/speaking-coach/guides/how-to-improve-public-speaking-skills/) works with both.

## Questions people ask

### Does Speeko have AI roleplay?

Yes. As of October 2026, its App Store page describes "Convos", interactive AI roleplays for interviews and everyday conversations.

### How much does Speeko cost?

The app is free to download with free exercises. On the US App Store in October 2026, Full Access is $24.99 a month or $99.99 a year, and an Access plan is $79.99 a year. Prices vary by country.

### Does Speeko have real human coaches?

Yes. Its coaching page offers 1:1 sessions starting with a $49.99 30-minute consultation, and its App Store notes mention group Live Events led by coaches. Speaking Coach has no human coaches.

### Which app is better for job interviews?

Both can help. Speeko has interview prompts and roleplays. Speaking Coach builds the mock interview from your own CV and the job post, and includes a 5-session plan for your interview day.

### Do they work in other languages?

Speeko's App Store page lists English, as of October 2026. Speaking Coach works in 20 languages, including Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Arabic, Japanese and Chinese.
