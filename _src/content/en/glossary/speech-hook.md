## Definition

People decide quickly whether a speaker is worth their attention. The hook is your first 10 to 30 seconds: the part designed to earn the rest. A good hook is short, specific and connected to your message. A hook that grabs attention but has nothing to do with the talk feels like a trick.

## Types of hook, with examples

These are made-up examples of each type.

| Type | Example hook |
| --- | --- |
| Question | "When did you last change your mind about something important?" |
| Short story | "At 6 a.m. on my first day as a nurse, a patient handed me a list of everything the night shift had got wrong." |
| Surprising detail | "Last month our support team answered 4,212 emails. Eleven of them said thank you." |
| Bold statement | "Most of our meetings should be emails." |
| Vivid image | "Picture the car park at 8:55 on a Monday: forty people circling for twelve spaces." |
| Callback to the room | "Everyone here walked past the same broken lift this morning." |

The [rhetorical question](/speaking-coach/glossary/rhetorical-question/) and the short [anecdote](/speaking-coach/glossary/anecdote/) are the most common choices because they work for almost any topic.

## Openings to avoid

| Opening | Why it falls flat | Instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Hi, my name is… and today I'll talk about…" | It spends your best seconds on admin | Hook first, name after |
| "Sorry, I'm a bit nervous" | It points attention at the nerves | Start with the first line you rehearsed |
| A joke unrelated to the topic | A laugh, then a gap before the real start | Use humour that leads to your point |
| "Webster's dictionary defines…" | Expected and slow | A story or question about the idea |

For full opening structures, see [how to start a speech](/speaking-coach/guides/how-to-start-a-speech/).

## Questions people ask

### How long should a speech hook be?

One to three sentences, usually under 30 seconds.

### Does every speech need a hook?

Every speech benefits from a strong first line. At a wedding or a work meeting it can be simple, but it shouldn't be an apology or admin.

### Can I use a quote as a hook?

You can, but check that the quote is real and attributed correctly, and connect it quickly to your point.

### Should the hook come before I introduce myself?

Often, yes. If someone has already introduced you, start with the hook straight away.
