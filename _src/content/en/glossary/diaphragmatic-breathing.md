## Definition

The diaphragm is the dome-shaped muscle under your lungs. When it contracts, it moves down and the lungs fill; your belly moves out to make room. Diaphragmatic breathing lets that happen fully instead of breathing high in the chest. Singers, actors and voice coaches use it as the base of voice support.

## Chest breathing vs belly breathing

| | Chest breathing | Diaphragmatic breathing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What moves | Upper chest and shoulders rise | Belly and lower ribs expand |
| Breath size | Often short and shallow | Fuller, slower |
| Voice | Can sound thin, tight or shaky | Steadier, easier to [project](/speaking-coach/glossary/vocal-projection/) |
| Common when | Nervous, rushed, slumped | Relaxed, upright |

Nerves tend to push breathing up into the chest, which is one reason a voice shakes before a speech. See [how to stop your voice shaking](/speaking-coach/guides/how-to-stop-voice-shaking/).

## A simple example exercise

Example practice, about 3 minutes:

1. Sit or stand upright with shoulders relaxed. Put one hand on your chest and one on your belly.
2. Breathe in through your nose for about 4 counts. Aim for the belly hand to move out while the chest hand stays mostly still.
3. Breathe out slowly on a gentle "sss" for about 6 to 8 counts.
4. Repeat 5 times.
5. Now breathe in the same way and count aloud from 1 to 10 on one breath, keeping the volume even.

When you speak, take that low breath at full stops and between ideas, not mid-sentence. [How to breathe while speaking](/speaking-coach/guides/how-to-breathe-while-speaking/) covers this in more detail.

Stop if you feel dizzy, and breathe normally. If you have a breathing condition or ongoing voice problems, ask a doctor or a qualified voice professional before starting exercises.

## Questions people ask

### Is diaphragmatic breathing the same as belly breathing?

Yes, in everyday use. The belly moves because the diaphragm pushes down on the organs below it.

### Does it help with nerves before speaking?

Many people find slow, low breathing calming. It's a general practice tip, not a treatment.

### How long does it take to learn?

The basic movement can be felt in a few minutes. Making it automatic while speaking takes regular practice.

### Should I breathe through my nose or mouth when speaking?

At natural pauses, a quick breath through the mouth is normal. The nose is easier for slow practice breaths.
