## Definition

Projection is about carrying, not volume alone. It combines three things: enough breath behind the sound, an open, relaxed throat and mouth, and clear [articulation](/speaking-coach/glossary/articulation/) so the words stay distinct at a distance. Shouting skips the first two and pushes from the throat, which tires the voice quickly.

## Projection vs shouting

| | Projecting | Shouting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Power comes from | Breath support, low in the body | Squeezing the throat |
| Sound | Full, warm, steady | Tight, harsh, often higher pitched |
| Can you keep it up? | For a whole talk | A few sentences before strain |
| Clarity | Words stay distinct | Words blur |
| Feels to the listener | Confident | Angry or panicked |

## Example

Example: you're giving a 10-minute talk to 40 people in a meeting room with no microphone.

1. Before people arrive, ask a colleague to stand in the back corner.
2. Say your first two sentences at what feels like a normal volume. Ask if every word was clear.
3. If not, take a lower breath ([diaphragmatic breathing](/speaking-coach/glossary/diaphragmatic-breathing/)), aim your voice at the back wall, and open your mouth a little more on vowels. Don't push from the throat.
4. Keep the last words of each sentence as strong as the first, because that's where projection usually drops.

## When you need it

- Rooms without a microphone
- Meetings where some people dial in from a speakerphone
- Noisy places: a party toast, a site briefing, a classroom
- Any time people keep asking you to repeat yourself

For technique and practice drills, see [how to project your voice without shouting](/speaking-coach/guides/how-to-project-your-voice/). If your voice often hurts or stays hoarse after speaking, see a doctor or a voice professional.

## Questions people ask

### Is projection the same as speaking loudly?

No. Projection is loud enough to carry, with control and clarity. Loud without support is closer to shouting.

### How do I know if I'm projecting enough?

Ask someone at the back of the room, or record from the far end and listen for missing word endings.

### Can quiet people learn to project?

Yes. Projection is a skill based on breath and openness, not personality.

### Does projecting mean I can't use a microphone?

Use the microphone when there is one. Projection still helps you sound clear and steady through it.
