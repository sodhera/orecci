## Meaning

"Deck" simply means a set of slides. A pitch deck is the visual half of a pitch; the other half is what you say. It is also called an investor deck, a startup deck or a pitch presentation. The term is used most for startups raising money, but also for the slides behind a sales pitch, a competition entry or a budget request at work.

## Types of pitch deck

| Type | Who sees it | How it differs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Presented investor deck | Investors in a meeting | Sparse slides; you do the explaining |
| Send-ahead or reading deck | Investors reading alone | More text, because it must make sense without you |
| Demo day or competition deck | A room or judging panel | A few minutes, so fewer slides |
| Sales deck | A potential customer | Problem, product and price; no fundraising ask |
| Internal pitch deck | Managers or a budget committee | Asks for budget, people or a go-ahead |

## Pitch deck example: slides and what to say

Example: a made-up company renting reusable takeaway boxes to restaurants; all numbers are invented. The third column is what you say. The slide holds the chart or screenshot that proves it.

| # | Slide | Example line to say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Title | "We rent reusable takeaway boxes to restaurants, so their packaging stops ending up in the bin." |
| 2 | Problem | "A busy takeaway buys around 2,000 single-use boxes a month, and the price has gone up twice this year." |
| 3 | Solution | "Customers return the box to any partner restaurant. We collect, wash and bring it back." |
| 4 | Product | "This is the restaurant dashboard: boxes out, boxes back, boxes due." |
| 5 | Market | "Our city has about 900 independent takeaways. We're starting with the 120 closest to our washing site." |
| 6 | Business model | "Restaurants pay a monthly fee per box in use, less than they spend on single-use boxes now." |
| 7 | Traction | "Eighteen restaurants pay us today, and 91 percent of boxes come back." |
| 8 | Competition | "The alternatives are cheap plastic or pricier compostable boxes. Ours get cheaper with every reuse." |
| 9 | Team | "I ran operations for a laundry chain. My co-founder built route planning for a courier firm." |
| 10 | Financials | "A restaurant becomes profitable for us in its fourth month." |
| 11 | The ask | "We're raising £400,000 to reach 150 restaurants by next autumn." |

If you can't say a slide in one sentence, it is probably doing two jobs. For the full spoken pitch and the questions investors ask, see [how to pitch to investors](/speaking-coach/presentations/investor-pitch/).

## Pitch deck vs. elevator pitch vs. business plan

| | Pitch deck | [Elevator pitch](/speaking-coach/glossary/elevator-pitch/) | Business plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Form | 10 to 15 slides plus your talk | Spoken, no slides | A long written document |
| Length | Often 10 to 20 minutes | 30 to 60 seconds | Many pages |
| Job | Win the money or the next meeting | Start a conversation | Show in detail how the business will run |

## Mistakes when presenting a pitch deck

| Mistake | Instead |
| --- | --- |
| Reading the slide word for word | Say the point; let the slide back it up |
| One huge market figure | Count real buyers you could reach |
| Saving your best number for slide 9 | If traction is your strongest point, move it up |
| Ending on "Any questions?" | End on the ask, then take questions |

## How to practise presenting your deck

1. Write one sentence per slide: what you'd say if you could say only one thing.
2. Run the deck out loud with a timer. About a minute per slide puts 11 slides near 12 minutes.
3. Practise jumping: have a friend call out "team" or "traction" mid-run and go straight there.
4. Learn the first 60 seconds well enough to say them without looking at the screen.
5. Do one run with someone playing an investor who interrupts. In Speaking Coach you can add your slides as a PDF, swipe through them while you speak, then answer the questions your audience would ask.

For the question round, see [how to handle Q&A](/speaking-coach/guides/how-to-handle-q-and-a/).

## Questions people ask

### What is a pitch deck in simple words?

A short slideshow that explains a business idea, usually to investors, while the founder talks them through it.

### How many slides should a pitch deck have?

Often 10 to 15. Fewer is fine if each slide earns its place; a demo day version can be shorter still.

### What is the difference between a pitch deck and a business plan?

A business plan is a long written document. A pitch deck is a short visual summary meant to get a meeting or support a conversation.

### How long should a pitch deck presentation be?

Often 10 to 20 minutes, but ask how long you have and plan to finish early. Prepare a 3-minute version for events.

### Should I send my pitch deck before the meeting?

Many founders send a fuller version ahead and present a cleaner one in the room. Ask what the investor prefers.
