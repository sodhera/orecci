## Mock interview apps compared

A question list is free everywhere. A mock interview app earns its place by doing what a list can't: asking about *your* experience, following up when you're vague, and fitting into the time you actually have. We make Speaking Coach, one of the apps below. We opened each other tool's own site or App Store page in November 2026 and state only what we saw; prices are as of November 2026.

| App | Best for | Free option | Platforms | What the AI does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Huru | Behavioral interviews on no budget | Free for job seekers | Web, iPhone, Chrome extension | Interviews you, follows up, scores each answer out of 100 |
| Speaking Coach app (ours) | Interviews built from your CV and the job post, on your phone | No free tier; a free AI voice review on this site | iPhone, Android | Interviews you from your CV and job post, follows up, quotes your words in its tips |
| Aced peer mocks | PM, coding, system design, finance | Monthly credits | Computer only | A human peer interviews you; AI grading is paid |
| interviewing.io | Senior engineers before a big-tech loop | Free AI Interviewer | Web | AI simulates coding and system design rounds; paid mocks are human |
| Big Interview | Learning a method from video lessons | Free iPhone app; site plans are paid | Its site, iPhone | AI feedback on recorded answers: content, communication, body language |
| Yoodli | Roleplays on a computer | 5 sessions for life | Web | Scored roleplays and speaking reports |

## The apps, one by one

**Huru.** A spoken AI interviewer with three pressure levels (Supportive, Standard, Demanding) and sessions of 5, 10, 15 or 20 minutes, by video or audio. Pick from 242+ roles, paste a job post, or write your own questions. Each answer is scored on relevance, structure, specificity, impact and articulation, and the debrief shows pace, filler and hedging words, long pauses and how often you say "I" rather than "we". Its iPhone listing says it is "100% free", with no paywall or trial, in five languages. **Pick it if** you want the most practice for $0. **Skip it if** you want it to read your full CV; its pages describe pasting a job post.

**The Speaking Coach app (ours).** You add your CV, the job post or your notes, and the AI interviewer asks about your actual experience, then follows up on what you said. Choose Gentle, Realistic or Tough; say "I need a moment" or "Help me" if you freeze. Afterwards you see your pace, filler words and long pauses, with tips that quote your own words, then retry the moment that went wrong. A 5-session plan leads up to your interview day, with a reminder the day before. It runs in 20 languages; your CV stays on your phone and only its text goes to the AI during interview sessions. It's a subscription; see the App Store or Google Play for the price in your country. **Skip it if** you need live coding practice, or if $0 matters more than CV-based questions: then use Huru.

**Aced (formerly Exponent).** Free peer mock interviews: you're matched with another candidate on video, about 30 minutes each way, in product management, data structures and algorithms, system design, behavioral or finance. Pramp is now Aced. Free users get monthly credits; AI feedback and transcripts need a paid membership, and mock interviews don't work on mobile. **Pick it if** you're a tech or PM candidate who wants a real person. **Skip it if** you can only practice on a phone.

**interviewing.io.** Anonymous, voice-only mocks with engineers from large tech companies, from $179 an interview with a full refund if you're unhappy, plus a free AI Interviewer for coding and system design. **Pick it if** you're preparing for a specific big-tech loop. **Skip it if** your interview is mostly behavioral.

**Big Interview.** A course as much as a simulator: 170 video lessons, an answer builder and AI feedback on "communication, body language, and content". Its site lists $39 for a month, $99 for three months or $299 lifetime, with a 30-day money-back guarantee. New since October: a free iPhone app for practicing common questions and recording answers. It's an early version (0.2.2 in October), with a 2.5 average from 4 ratings when we looked. The home page doesn't say whether it reads your CV or asks follow-ups. **Pick it if** you want to be taught a method, or your university pays for it. **Skip it if** you want a conversation rather than recorded answers.

**Yoodli.** Mostly sold to companies now; its home page mentions AI interview coaching for executive search and career changes. Individuals get 5 free lifetime sessions, then Pro at $8 a month billed yearly. **Pick it if** you also want presentation practice on the web.

## Why we left out live interview copilots

Final Round AI's home page describes Interview CoPilot, a desktop app that listens during a live interview and privately suggests answers, on its Pro plan from $25 a month. It also has an AI practice interviewer with eight scenarios, so it isn't only a copilot. We've left copilots off this list for two reasons:

1. **They don't make you better.** Reading an answer during the interview doesn't build the skill you need for the next one.
2. **They can cost you the offer.** An employer that notices hidden live help may treat it as cheating.

If you're moving away from one, see our [Final Round AI alternatives](/speaking-coach/compare/final-round-ai-alternatives/).

## How to choose

| If your interview is... | Practice with |
| --- | --- |
| Behavioral, and money is tight | Huru, plus the free AI voice review for single answers |
| Behavioral, and you want questions about your real CV on your phone | The Speaking Coach app |
| Coding or system design | interviewing.io's AI Interviewer, then a human mock on Aced or interviewing.io |
| Product management | Aced peer mocks |
| Your first ever, and you want lessons | Big Interview |
| In Spanish, French, German or Portuguese | Huru or the Speaking Coach app; for other languages, the Speaking Coach app |

Whichever you pick:

1. Do one session cold, before you prepare. It shows which questions you can't answer.
2. Prepare stories for those questions using the [STAR method](/speaking-coach/guides/star-method/).
3. Do two or three sessions on the hardest pressure setting.
4. The day before, do one short, gentle session. Confidence matters more than new material now.
5. For technical rounds, add at least one mock with a real person; our [technical interview guide](/speaking-coach/interviews/technical-interview/) covers why.

## Questions people ask

### What is the best free mock interview app?

As of November 2026, Huru says it is 100% free for job seekers, and Aced offers free peer mocks with monthly credits. Our [free interview practice tools](/speaking-coach/best/best-free-interview-practice-tools/) list gives the exact limit of each.

### Can a mock interview app read my resume?

Some can. The Speaking Coach app reads your CV, the job post or your notes. Huru builds questions from a job post you paste. Big Interview's home page doesn't say.

### Are AI mock interviews as good as practicing with a person?

They're better for volume: you can do ten at midnight. A person is better at spotting what sounds rehearsed. Use AI for repetitions and a person at least once before an important interview.

### Which app is best for coding interviews?

Aced's peer mocks and interviewing.io focus on coding and system design. Huru, Big Interview and the Speaking Coach app are built for spoken answers, not live coding.

### Is using an AI copilot during a real interview allowed?

It's usually not disclosed, and many employers would see it as cheating. Practice tools are used before the interview, so that risk doesn't apply.
