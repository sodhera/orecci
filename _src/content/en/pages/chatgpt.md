## What the ChatGPT app does

Speaking Coach in ChatGPT is a **voice review**. You speak; it listens to your recording and tells you how it sounded and what to change. It reviews your own voice: what it measures comes from your recording, not from text you type.

Each review gives you:

- **Your pace** in words per minute.
- **Your filler words**, such as "um", "like" and "you know".
- **Your pauses**, including the long ones.
- **Feedback that quotes your spoken words**, so you can see exactly which sentence to fix.
- **One change to try.** Then you can try again and compare the second attempt with the first.

It is free. It doesn't rate your accent, pronunciation or personality, and it makes no medical assessment.

## How to use it, step by step

1. **Open ChatGPT** and type **@Speaking Coach**, or find Speaking Coach under Apps.
2. **Say what you are practicing** in one line, for example the interview question or the speech.
3. **Optionally add a document** as background: a PDF, Word, PowerPoint, text or Markdown file. Only its text is used, so a CV, a job post or your speech notes all work.
4. **Record or upload your voice.** Answer the question or give the part of the speech you are working on. A minute or two is enough to see a pattern.
5. **Read the review.** Start with the quoted line and the one change to try.
6. **Try again** with that one change and compare the second attempt. Change one thing at a time; fixing everything at once usually fixes nothing.

## Example prompts to type

These are examples. Swap in your own question, role or occasion.

> @Speaking Coach review my answer to "Tell me about yourself" for a junior data analyst job.

> @Speaking Coach I've attached the job post. Review my answer to "Why should we hire you?" and quote the weakest sentence.

> @Speaking Coach review the first minute of my best man speech. I want fewer ums.

> @Speaking Coach I'm presenting a project update tomorrow. Review my opening and tell me if my pace is too fast.

> @Speaking Coach here's my second attempt. Compare it with the first one.

If you need questions to answer first, the [interview questions](/speaking-coach/interview-questions/) pages list common ones with example answers, such as [tell me about yourself](/speaking-coach/interview-questions/tell-me-about-yourself/).

## The limits

| Limit | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| English only | Record in English. For other languages, use the phone app, which works in 20. |
| Two reviews per network connection per day | People on the same Wi-Fi or connection may share the two. Plan which answers matter most. |
| Shared capacity | The free reviews draw on capacity shared by everyone, so a review can be unavailable at busy times. |
| One optional document, text only | Images, charts and slide design are not read. |
| Your recording, not a conversation | It reviews what you recorded. For a partner who asks follow-up questions, use the phone app. |

Because you only get two a day, use the first on your weakest answer and the second on your retry. For extra rounds of counting on your own, the [free speech review](/speaking-coach/tools/free-speech-review/) on this site measures pace, fillers and pauses in your browser, without AI feedback.

## How it differs from the phone app

| | Free in ChatGPT | Speaking Coach app (iPhone and Android) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you do | Record or upload your own voice | Practice out loud with an AI voice partner who plays the other side and follows up on what you said |
| Situations | Anything you record | 23 built-in scenes, such as Tell me about yourself, Ask for a raise and First date, or any situation you describe |
| Feedback | Pace, fillers, pauses, quoted feedback, one change to try | Pace, fillers, long pauses or freezes, how quickly you started, what to keep, and "You said / Try" tips |
| Retry | Try again and compare | Retry this moment: 90 seconds on the same moment, then see what changed |
| Documents | One optional file, text only | Your CV, job post or notes for mock interviews; slides as a PDF for presentations |
| Languages | English | 20 |
| Cost | Free, two reviews per network connection per day | Subscription; see the App Store or Google Play for the price in your country |

The ChatGPT app is the quick check: did that answer sound the way you meant it to? The phone app is for practicing the whole conversation, with pressure levels from Gentle to Tough, and a partner who asks the next question. The [how we review](/speaking-coach/how-we-review/) page explains what the measures mean and where they fall short.

## Questions people ask

### Is Speaking Coach in ChatGPT really free?

Yes. The voice review in ChatGPT is free, with two reviews per network connection per day, subject to shared capacity.

### Can I use it in Spanish, Hindi or another language?

Not in ChatGPT, which works in English only. The phone app works in 20 languages, including Spanish, Hindi, Arabic and Japanese.

### Will it judge my accent?

No. It doesn't assess accent, pronunciation or personality. It focuses on pace, filler words, pauses and the words you used.

### Can I upload my CV or speech?

Yes, as an optional background document: PDF, Word, PowerPoint, text or Markdown. Only the text is used.

### Is it the same as using ChatGPT's voice mode?

No. Speaking Coach measures your recording and quotes your own words back to you with one change to try. See [Speaking Coach vs ChatGPT voice mode](/speaking-coach/compare/speaking-coach-vs-chatgpt-voice-mode/) for a fuller comparison.
