## Practice out loud, not in your head

Reading an answer and saying it are different skills. Most people know what they want to say in an interview or a toast. It falls apart when they have to say it, with someone looking at them, in real time.

Speaking Coach is built for that moment. In the app, an AI voice partner plays the other side: your interviewer, your audience, your manager, or a stranger who says hi. It listens, follows up on what you actually said, and afterwards shows how you sounded and one change to try. Then you can retry the exact moment that tripped you up.

## What you'll find here

- **Interview questions:** what each common question is really asking, example answers for different levels of experience, and the mistakes to avoid.
- **Questions by role:** what interviewers ask nurses, software engineers, teachers, sales reps and many more.
- **Speeches and presentations:** structure, timing and example lines for toasts, eulogies, pitches and talks.
- **Small talk:** openers and follow-up questions for networking events, new jobs and parties.
- **Free tools:** a filler word counter, a pace checker and a speech length calculator that run in your browser.
